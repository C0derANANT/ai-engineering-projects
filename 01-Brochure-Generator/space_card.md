# 🏢 AI Company Brochure Generator

Enter a company name and its website. The app reads the site, writes a brochure
with an LLM (Groq, `openai/gpt-oss-120b`) in your chosen language, tone and
sections, and exports it as a styled PDF and Word file in your brand colour.

Source code and setup: [GitHub](https://github.com/C0derANANT/ai-engineering-projects/tree/main/01-Brochure-Generator)
