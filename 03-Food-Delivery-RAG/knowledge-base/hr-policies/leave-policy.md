# Leave Policy

_Effective: 1 April 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: People Team (HR) · Policy ID: HR-POL-004_

## 1. Scope

- Applies to all **full-time employees** of CraveCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. in India, including employees on probation.
- **Interns** receive 1 day of paid leave per month of internship and public holidays only.
- **Fixed-term contract staff** follow this policy, pro-rated to their contract length, unless their contract says otherwise.
- **Does not apply to** delivery partners (independent contractors) or restaurant partner staff.
- Where state Shops and Establishments Acts or other laws give more generous entitlements, the law prevails.

## 2. Leave year and the system of record

- The leave year runs from **1 April to 31 March**.
- All leave is applied for, approved and tracked in **CravePeople** (the HR portal and app). Leave not recorded in CravePeople is treated as unauthorised absence.
- Balances are shown in days. Half-day leave (first or second half) is allowed for Earned, Casual and Sick Leave.

## 4. Earned Leave (EL)

- Accrues at **1.5 days per completed month** of service, from the joining date.
- **Probation:** EL accrues from day one but can be **used only after 3 months** of service, except in emergencies approved by the HR Business Partner (HRBP).
- **Notice:** apply at least **7 calendar days in advance** for EL of more than 3 consecutive days, and **2 working days in advance** for shorter EL.
- **Carry forward:** the EL balance is capped at **30 days**. Any balance above 30 days on 31 March lapses.
- **Encashment:** only at exit, for up to 30 days, calculated on **basic salary** (basic ÷ 30 × days).
- Weekends and public holidays that fall within an EL period are **not** counted as leave (sandwich rule does not apply).

**Example:** Ananya joins on 10 June 2026. By 10 September she has 4.5 days of EL and can now use it. By 31 March 2027 she will have accrued 13.5 days.

## 5. Casual Leave (CL)

- **2 days credited at the start of each quarter** (April, July, October, January). New joiners get the full 2 days if they join in the first month of a quarter, 1 day in the second month, and none in the third month.
- For short personal needs. **A maximum of 3 consecutive days**, and CL cannot be combined with EL to extend a vacation.
- Unused CL lapses at the end of the leave year. It is not encashable.
- Can be applied for on the same day, with a message to the manager before the start of the working day.

## 6. Sick Leave (SL)

- **10 days** credited on 1 April (pro-rated for mid-year joiners).
- **Medical certificate required** for more than **2 consecutive days** of SL, uploaded to CravePeople within 3 working days of returning.
- SL balance carries forward up to a total of **20 days**.
- **Extended illness:** if SL and EL are exhausted, the employee may take up to 30 days of LWP. The HRBP may approve an additional **Special Medical Leave** of up to 15 days with pay in cases of hospitalisation or serious illness. This needs CHRO approval.
- SL may also be used to care for a **dependent family member** (spouse or partner, children, parents) who is ill.

## 7. Public holidays

- **10 fixed holidays** are published by HR in March for the coming year, set by office location (they vary by state).
- **2 optional holidays** can be chosen from a list of 8 published festivals.
- **Customer support, operations and on-call teams** work on a roster. Employees rostered on a public holiday get **one Compensatory Off** plus a **holiday shift allowance of ₹1,500**.

## 8. Maternity, paternity and adoption leave

### 8.1 Maternity Leave

- **26 weeks of fully paid leave** for the first two children, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961. Up to 8 weeks can be taken before the expected delivery date.
- **12 weeks** for the third child onwards.
- **6 weeks** in case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, on medical certification.
- **Eligibility:** available from day one of employment. The legal requirement of 80 days' service is waived by CraveCart.
- **After maternity leave:** employees may opt for **work from home for up to 3 months**, subject to role suitability, and a **phased return** at 60% hours for 4 weeks at full pay.
- Maternity leave **does not affect** performance ratings, increments or promotion eligibility.

### 8.2 Paternity Leave

- **15 working days** of paid leave, to be taken within **6 months** of the child's birth, in up to two blocks.
- Also applies to non-birthing partners in same-sex relationships.

### 8.3 Adoption and surrogacy

- **Primary caregiver:** 12 weeks of paid leave when adopting a child under 3 months old (as per the law), and **12 weeks** for children up to 12 years old, extended by CraveCart policy.
- **Commissioning mother (surrogacy):** 26 weeks.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 15 working days.

## 9. Bereavement Leave

- **5 working days** of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member: spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law or grandparent.
- **2 working days** for other relatives.
- Can be taken immediately without prior approval. Inform the manager as soon as possible, and record it in CravePeople within 7 days of returning.

## 10. Marriage Leave

- **5 working days** of paid leave for the employee's own wedding, once during employment.
- To be taken within 1 month before or 3 months after the wedding date.

## 11. Compensatory Off (Comp-off)

- Earned when an employee works a full day on a weekly off or public holiday **with prior written approval** from their manager.
- **Half-day comp-off** for 4 to 6 hours worked.
- Must be used within **60 days**, otherwise it lapses. It cannot be encashed.
- Employees at **Grade L6 and above** are not eligible for comp-off but may take time off flexibly with their manager's agreement.

## 12. Wellness Days

- Two company-wide paid days off each year (in 2026: **14 August** and **26 December**), when the corporate offices close.
- Customer support and operations teams on these days take them on an alternative date within 30 days.

## 13. Sabbatical

- Employees with **3 or more years** of continuous service may request an **unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months** for study, caregiving, health or personal projects.
- Apply at least **60 days in advance**. It needs manager, department head and HRBP approval.
- Health insurance continues during the sabbatical. ESOP vesting is **paused** for the sabbatical period.
- The employee returns to the same or an equivalent role.

## 14. Leave Without Pay (LWP)

- Granted when the relevant paid leave is exhausted, for up to **30 days in a leave year**, with HRBP approval.
- Salary is deducted pro-rata: (monthly gross ÷ calendar days in the month) × LWP days.
- **Unauthorised absence** (no approval and no communication) for **3 or more consecutive working days** is treated as LWP and may lead to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct. **8 or more consecutive days** may be treated as job abandonment.

## 15. Applying for and approving leave

1. Apply in CravePeople with the dates, leave type and a short reason (a reason is optional for EL and CL).
2. The manager approves or rejects within **3 working days**. If the manager doesn't act, the request **auto-escalates to the skip-level manager**.
3. Rejections must include a reason and, where possible, alternative dates.
4. **Emergency leave:** inform your manager by call or message before your start time and apply in CravePeople within 2 working days of returning.
5. Cancelling approved leave: possible in CravePeople before the leave starts. After the leave starts, ask the manager to amend it.

## 16. Blackout periods (operations and support roles)

To protect service during peak demand, EL and CL requests for **customer support, operations, dispatch and on-call engineering** roles need **department head approval** during:

- Diwali week (the 3 days around Diwali),
- 31 December and 1 January,
- the final match days of major cricket tournaments,
- any period declared as a peak event by Operations, with 30 days' notice.

Sick, bereavement, maternity and paternity leave are never subject to blackout.

## 17. Leave during probation and notice period

- **Probation (first 6 months):** CL and SL can be used from day one. EL can be used after 3 months.
- **Notice period:** employees may use EL during notice with manager approval. EL **cannot** be used to shorten the notice period unless the department head approves. Remaining EL (up to 30 days) is encashed at the final settlement.
- LWP taken during the notice period extends the last working day correspondingly, unless waived.

## 18. Frequently asked questions

**Q: I joined on 20 January. How much leave do I have on 31 March?**
A: EL: 3 days (1.5 days on completing each month of service, on 20 February and 20 March). CL: 2 days (you joined in the first month of the January–March quarter, so you get the full quarterly credit). SL: pro-rated for the 3 months left in the leave year, 10 × 3/12 = 2.5 days. You can use CL and SL now. EL becomes usable from 20 April, after 3 months of service.

**Q: Can I take 10 days of EL in a row?**
A: Yes, if you have the balance and apply at least 7 days in advance. Long leave (more than 10 working days) should be planned with your manager a month ahead where possible.

**Q: My child is sick. Can I use my sick leave?**
A: Yes. SL can be used to care for an ill dependant.

**Q: What happens to my EL balance if I'm promoted or move teams?**
A: Balances carry over unchanged.

**Q: I worked on Holi because of a production incident, without prior approval.**
A: Comp-off normally needs prior approval, but for on-call incidents, approval from the incident commander or manager **within 48 hours afterwards** is accepted.
