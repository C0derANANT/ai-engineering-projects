# Employee Benefits

_Effective: 1 April 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: People Team (Total Rewards) · Policy ID: HR-POL-006_

## 1. Scope and eligibility

- Applies to **full-time employees** of CraveCart in India from their **date of joining**, unless a benefit states otherwise.
- **Interns:** health insurance (employee only), meal benefit and the Employee Assistance Programme.
- **Fixed-term contractors:** as set out in their contract (usually health insurance and meal benefit).
- **Not covered:** delivery partners and restaurant partner staff. Delivery partners have a separate insurance and welfare programme.
- Benefits are reviewed every year in March. Changes are announced at least 30 days in advance.

## 3. Health insurance

### 3.1 Group Medical Cover (GMC)

- **Sum insured:** ₹5 lakh per year, **family floater**.
- **Covered members:** employee, spouse or partner (including same-sex and live-in partners, declared in CravePeople), and up to **2 children** (up to age 25). Children with disabilities are covered regardless of age.
- **Premium:** fully paid by CraveCart.
- **Day-one cover:** no waiting period. **Pre-existing diseases are covered** from day one.
- **Maternity:** covered up to **₹75,000** (normal or caesarean), including newborn cover from day one.
- **Room rent:** single private room, no sub-limit.
- **Cashless** at network hospitals through the insurer's third-party administrator (TPA). For non-network hospitals, claims are reimbursed.
- **Mental health:** in-patient psychiatric treatment covered up to the sum insured.
- **Not covered:** cosmetic treatment, experimental treatment, and the standard exclusions listed in the policy document on CravePeople.

### 3.2 Parents' cover (optional)

- Employees can add **parents or parents-in-law** (one set) with a separate ₹3 lakh floater.
- The premium is paid by the employee at group rates (about ₹14,000 to ₹22,000 per year depending on age), deducted from salary in 12 instalments.
- Enrolment is allowed only **within 30 days of joining** or during the **annual enrolment window** (March).

### 3.3 Top-up and OPD

- **Voluntary top-up:** an extra ₹10 lakh above the base cover. The employee pays the premium (about ₹3,500 per year for the family).
- **OPD cover:** ₹10,000 per year per family for doctor consultations, diagnostics and prescribed medicines, paid by CraveCart and claimed through the TPA app.

### 3.4 Adding or changing dependants

- New joiners add dependants within **30 days** of joining.
- **Life events** (marriage, birth, adoption) must be declared within **30 days** of the event, outside the annual window.
- Missing the window means the dependant can be added only at the next annual enrolment.

## 4. Life and accident insurance

- **Group Term Life:** **3× annual fixed CTC** (minimum ₹20 lakh), paid to the nominee in case of the employee's death for any reason.
- **Group Personal Accident:** **5× annual fixed CTC** (minimum ₹25 lakh) for accidental death or permanent disability. Partial disability is paid on a scale.
- Both are fully paid by CraveCart. **Nominations must be updated in CravePeople.**

## 5. Retirement benefits

### 5.1 Provident Fund

- Employee and employer each contribute **12% of basic salary**.
- By default, contributions are calculated on the **full basic salary**. Employees may choose the statutory wage ceiling (₹15,000 per month) in the flexible benefits plan at joining or each April.
- Existing UANs are linked at joining. Transfers from previous employers happen online through the EPFO portal.

### 5.2 Gratuity

- Paid at exit after **5 years of continuous service** (treated as completed at **4 years and 240 days**), as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.
- Formula: (last drawn basic + DA) × 15/26 × completed years of service.
- The 5-year condition doesn't apply in case of death or disability.

### 5.3 National Pension System (NPS)

- Optional. Employees may route up to **10% of basic** as an employer NPS contribution through the flexible benefits plan, for tax efficiency under the new tax regime.

## 6. Meal benefit

- **₹3,000 per month** in **CraveCart employee credits**, loaded on the 1st of each month.
- Can be used on any CraveCart order, at any time, including for family.
- Credits **expire at the end of each month** and are not carried forward or encashable.
- Office cafeterias offer subsidised breakfast and lunch on office days. The cafeteria is separate from the ₹3,000 credit.
- **Misuse:** using credits for resale, or transferring them to non-employees for money, is a Code of Conduct violation.

## 7. Learning and development

- **₹30,000 per financial year** for courses, certifications, books, conferences and online learning platforms related to the employee's current or a likely future role.
- Pro-rated for mid-year joiners (for example, joining in October gives ₹15,000 for that year).
- Needs **manager approval before spending**. Claims are made with invoices within 60 days.
- **Clawback:** for any single course or certification costing more than ₹50,000 (approved above the budget by the department head), the employee repays pro-rata if they resign within 12 months of completion.
- **Internal learning:** CraveCart Academy runs free courses on product, data, leadership and AI. **5 learning days** a year can be used for approved learning without taking leave.

## 8. Wellness

- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** free, confidential 24x7 counselling by phone, chat or video, with **6 sessions per year** for the employee and each covered family member. CraveCart never sees who uses it.
- **Fitness:** gym, yoga, sports or fitness app subscriptions reimbursed up to **₹1,500 per month**, claimed quarterly.
- **Annual health check-up:** free for employees aged **30 and above**, and for spouses or partners aged **35 and above**.
- **Wellness Days:** two company-wide days off (see `hr-policies/leave-policy.md`).

## 9. Work-from-home and connectivity

- **Internet and phone:** up to **₹1,500 per month** reimbursed against bills (part of the flexible benefits plan).
- **Home office setup:** a one-time **₹10,000** allowance for a chair, desk or monitor after confirmation. The items belong to the employee.
- **On-call allowance (engineering and operations):** ₹2,000 per on-call week, plus ₹1,000 per incident outside working hours requiring more than 1 hour of work.

## 10. Parental and family benefits

- **Maternity, paternity and adoption leave:** see `hr-policies/leave-policy.md`.
- **Childcare reimbursement:** up to **₹8,000 per month per child** under 6 years, for a creche or day care, available to **all parents** regardless of gender.
- **New parent gift:** a ₹5,000 CraveCart gift card and a baby care kit.
- **Fertility and adoption support:** reimbursement of up to **₹1,00,000 once during employment** for fertility treatment (IVF, IUI) or legal adoption expenses, for employees with 1 or more years of service.

## 11. Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

- **Eligibility:** all employees at **Grade L5 and above**, and selected critical roles at other grades, as stated in the offer letter or a refresh grant.
- **Vesting:** **4 years** with a **1-year cliff**. 25% vests on the first anniversary of the grant date, and the remainder vests **quarterly** (6.25% per quarter) over the next 3 years.
- **Exercise:** vested options can be exercised during employment at the exercise windows published by Finance, and for **5 years after leaving** (except termination for cause, where unvested and vested-unexercised options lapse).
- **Leave of absence:** vesting pauses during sabbaticals and leave without pay of more than 30 days.
- **Taxes:** a perquisite tax applies on exercise. Finance holds ESOP tax sessions each January.
- ESOP details are **confidential** (see `hr-policies/code-of-conduct.md`).

## 12. Referral bonus

| Role hired | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Engineering, data science, product, design | ₹50,000 |
| Grade L6 and above (any function) | ₹50,000 |
| All other corporate roles | ₹25,000 |
| Customer support (bulk hiring) | ₹5,000 |

- Paid after the referred hire completes **90 days**. The referrer must still be employed then.
- Not paid for referring someone into your own reporting line, or for candidates already in the recruitment system in the last 6 months.

## 13. Recognition and long service

- **Spot awards:** managers can give ₹5,000 to ₹25,000 for outstanding work, approved by the skip-level manager.
- **Quarterly "Customer First" awards:** peer-nominated, ₹25,000 and a trophy.
- **Long service:** 3 years: ₹15,000 voucher. 5 years: ₹50,000 voucher and 5 extra days of EL. 10 years: ₹1,50,000 voucher and a 2-week paid break.

## 14. Flexible Benefits Plan (FBP)

- Part of fixed CTC can be structured into tax-efficient components: meal cards, telephone and internet, fuel and vehicle (where applicable), books and periodicals, NPS, and Leave Travel Allowance (LTA).
- Choose components in CravePeople at joining and each **April** (one change allowed mid-year for life events).
- Unclaimed FBP amounts are paid as taxable salary in March.

## 15. When benefits end

- **Health insurance:** coverage ends on the **last working day**. Employees can convert to an individual policy with the insurer within **30 days** of exit without new waiting periods, at their own cost.
- **Life and accident insurance:** end on the last working day.
- **Meal credits, fitness and internet:** pro-rated to the last working day.
- **Learning and relocation clawbacks** (if any) are recovered in the full and final settlement.
- **ESOPs:** see section 11.
- **Death of an employee:** CraveCart continues the **family's health insurance for 2 years** at no cost and gives the family a one-time support payment of **₹2,00,000** in addition to insurance and statutory dues.

## 16. Frequently asked questions

**Q: My partner and I aren't married. Can I add them to my health insurance?**
A: Yes. Declare them as a partner in CravePeople within 30 days of joining or at the annual window.

**Q: Can I use my meal credits to order for my parents?**
A: Yes, as long as the order is placed from your account.

**Q: I joined in September. How much learning budget do I have this year?**
A: 7/12 of ₹30,000 = ₹17,500 (September to March).

**Q: My child was born last week. What should I do?**
A: Add the baby to your GMC in CravePeople within 30 days, apply for paternity or maternity leave, and claim your new parent gift. The newborn is covered from birth.

**Q: Are delivery partners covered by these benefits?**
A: No. Delivery partners are independent contractors with their own programme: accident insurance of ₹10 lakh, hospitalisation cover of ₹1 lakh, and loss-of-pay cover during injury, administered by Fleet Operations.
