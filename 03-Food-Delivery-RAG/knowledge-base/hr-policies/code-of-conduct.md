# Employee Code of Conduct

_Effective: 1 April 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: People Team and Legal · Policy ID: HR-POL-001_

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Code sets the standard of behaviour expected of everyone who works at CraveCart. It applies to all employees, interns, trainees and fixed-term contractors, at work, at company events, while travelling for work, and online when representing CraveCart or interacting with colleagues.

Every employee must **read and acknowledge** this Code in CravePeople within **14 days of joining**, and again each year in April.

If this Code conflicts with the law, the law prevails. If you're unsure whether something is acceptable, ask your manager, your HR Business Partner (HRBP) or `ethics@cravecart.example` **before** acting.

## 2. Our values in practice

| Value | What it looks like | What it doesn't look like |
|---|---|---|
| Customer First | Fixing the customer's problem even when it's not "your" team's | "That's not my department" |
| Own It | Raising a risk early, admitting mistakes, closing the loop | Hiding bad news, blaming others |
| Move Fast, Safely | Shipping in small steps with tests and rollback plans | Skipping reviews to hit a deadline; pushing delivery partners to ride unsafely |
| Respect Everyone | Polite to delivery partners, restaurant staff, support agents and interns alike | Talking down to frontline staff; mocking accents or backgrounds |
| Be Honest | Accurate numbers, transparent fees, truthful ETAs | Inflating metrics, misleading customers |

## 3. Respectful workplace

### 3.1 Anti-discrimination and equal opportunity

CraveCart does not discriminate on the basis of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, caste, religion, ethnicity, region, language, disability, age, marital or parental status, or HIV status. This applies to hiring, pay, promotion, assignments and exit. Reasonable accommodations are provided for employees with disabilities and for religious practice, where feasible.

### 3.2 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

- CraveCart follows the **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013**. As company policy, protection extends to **all genders**.
- **Sexual harassment** includes unwelcome physical contact, demands or requests for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, showing pornography, and any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature, including over chat, email and social media.
- **Internal Committee (IC):** each office has an IC with an external member. The members are listed on CravePeople > Policies > POSH.
- **How to complain:** in writing to the IC at `ic@cravecart.example`, within **3 months** of the incident (extendable by another 3 months for valid reasons).
- **Process:** the IC may attempt conciliation if the complainant requests it. Otherwise it holds an inquiry and completes it within **90 days**, then shares its report within 10 days. Action is taken within 60 days of the report.
- **Confidentiality** is mandatory. Sharing details of a POSH case is itself a violation.
- **Mandatory training:** POSH awareness within 14 days of joining, and a yearly refresher.

### 3.3 Bullying and harassment (non-sexual)

Shouting, public humiliation, threats, deliberate exclusion, repeated unreasonable criticism and abusive messages are not tolerated. Complaints go to the HRBP or the ethics hotline and are investigated within **30 days**.

### 3.4 Interacting with delivery partners and restaurant partners

- Treat delivery partners and restaurant staff with the same respect as colleagues.
- Never use internal access to look up a delivery partner's or restaurant owner's personal details for non-work reasons.
- Employees doing ride-alongs or restaurant visits must follow the field safety guidelines: helmet when riding pillion, no filming without consent, and no interfering with the delivery.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when your personal interests could influence, or appear to influence, your decisions at CraveCart.

**You must disclose in CravePeople within 30 days** (or before taking any related decision):

- any ownership, investment or role in a restaurant, cloud kitchen, delivery company, food brand or CraveCart vendor,
- a close relative or partner working at a competitor, restaurant partner or vendor,
- a romantic relationship with someone in your reporting line (the reporting line is changed, with no penalty, when it's disclosed),
- outside employment, consulting or board roles.

**Not allowed without written approval from the CHRO and Legal:**

- working for or advising a competitor (other food or grocery delivery platforms),
- moonlighting during work hours or using CraveCart equipment for outside work.

Personal projects, teaching, writing and open-source work outside work hours, unrelated to CraveCart's business and using no CraveCart information, are generally allowed. Disclose them if unsure.

**Example:** a Restaurant Operations associate's brother opens a cloud kitchen in the city she manages. She discloses it. Her manager reassigns that restaurant's account to a colleague. No conflict remains.

## 5. Gifts, hospitality and anti-bribery

- You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to **₹2,000 per source per year** (for example, festival sweets or a branded notebook). Anything more must be declined or declared and handed to HR.
- **Never** accept cash, gift cards, vouchers, loans or personal discounts from restaurant partners, vendors or delivery partners.
- **Restaurant-facing roles:** tasting food during an official visit is fine. Free meals for personal or family consumption are not.
- **Never offer** anything of value to government officials, inspectors or anyone else to obtain a licence, approval or business advantage. CraveCart has zero tolerance for bribery under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
- Facilitation payments ("speed money") are prohibited, even if small or customary.

## 6. Data protection, confidentiality and security

### 6.1 Customer and partner data

- CraveCart processes personal data under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**.
- Access customer, delivery partner and restaurant data **only when your role needs it**. All access through the Agent Console and internal tools is logged and audited.
- **Never:**
  - look up friends, family, celebrities or ex-partners in internal tools,
  - export customer personal data to personal devices, personal email or unapproved tools (including public AI chatbots),
  - share screenshots that contain personal data outside approved channels,
  - ask customers for OTPs, passwords, card numbers or UPI PINs.

### 6.2 Confidential company information

- Unreleased features, financials, fundraising, restaurant commission rates, ESOP details and strategy documents are **confidential**.
- Don't discuss confidential matters in public places, on social media, or with friends who work at competitors.
- Confidentiality obligations continue **after you leave** CraveCart.

### 6.3 Information security

- Use company-managed devices with disk encryption and screen lock (5 minutes maximum).
- Use multi-factor authentication on all work accounts. Never share passwords.
- Report lost devices, phishing emails or suspected breaches to `security@cravecart.example` within **2 hours**.
- Install only approved software. Use only approved AI tools for work data.

## 7. Using the CraveCart platform as an employee

- Employees may use CraveCart as customers, with the employee meal benefit (see `hr-policies/benefits.md`).
- **Never** use internal access to issue refunds, coupons, CraveCart Money or Plus benefits to yourself, family or friends. This is treated as **gross misconduct** and fraud, regardless of the amount.
- Employees must not post reviews of restaurants they manage or have a relationship with.

## 8. Social media, public speaking and the media

- Only **Corporate Communications** may speak to the media for CraveCart. Send all media enquiries to `press@cravecart.example`.
- On personal social media you may say you work at CraveCart, but make it clear your views are your own. Don't share confidential information, customer data or internal screenshots.
- Don't respond to customer complaints on social media from personal accounts. Share the post with the Social Care team.
- Speaking at external events about CraveCart's work needs approval from your department head and Corporate Communications.

## 9. Attendance and working arrangements

- **Corporate roles:** hybrid model, with **3 days a week in office** (Tuesday to Thursday by default; teams may agree different anchor days). Core collaboration hours are **11:00 am to 4:00 pm IST**.
- **Support and operations roles:** shift rosters published 2 weeks ahead. Swaps need lead approval in the Workforce Management tool.
- Be on time for shifts. **3 late log-ins (15 minutes or more) in a month** for rostered roles leads to a conversation with the team lead. Repeated lateness may lead to a written warning.
- **Remote work from outside India** needs advance approval (tax and data-security reasons) and is limited to 30 days per year.

## 10. Health, safety and substance use

- Being under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs at work, during shifts or while on call is prohibited.
- At company events where alcohol is served, drink responsibly. Misconduct at events is treated the same as misconduct at work.
- Report workplace injuries and unsafe conditions to Facilities or HR within 24 hours.
- Smoking is allowed only in designated areas.

## 11. Company assets

- Laptops, phones, access cards and software licences remain CraveCart property and must be returned on the last working day.
- Reasonable personal use of devices is allowed, but company devices may be monitored for security purposes, in line with the law.
- Expense claims must be honest and supported by receipts. Inflated or fake claims are gross misconduct.

## 12. Reporting concerns (whistleblower policy)

- Report suspected violations of this Code, fraud, safety risks or legal breaches to any of:
  - your manager or skip-level manager,
  - your HRBP,
  - the **Ethics Hotline**, run by an independent third party (web form or toll-free number on CravePeople), which can be **anonymous**,
  - `ethics@cravecart.example`, reviewed by the Ethics Committee (CHRO, General Counsel and an independent director).
- Reports are acknowledged within **3 working days**, and investigations aim to finish within **45 days**.
- **No retaliation:** anyone who reports in good faith is protected. Retaliation, including demotion, exclusion or a poor rating because of a report, is itself gross misconduct.
- Knowingly false reports made to harm others are a violation.

## 13. Disciplinary process

### 13.1 Categories of misconduct

| Category | Examples | Typical outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | Occasional lateness, minor policy lapses, not completing mandatory training on time | Verbal warning, coaching |
| **Major** | Repeated minor misconduct, unauthorised absence of 3 or more days, misusing company assets, disrespectful behaviour, undisclosed conflict of interest | Written warning, final written warning, withholding of increment or bonus |
| **Gross** | Fraud (including self-issued refunds or fake expense claims), theft, sexual harassment, violence or threats, accepting bribes, deliberate data leaks, working for a competitor, being under the influence at work, retaliation | Suspension pending inquiry, then termination; may be reported to authorities |

### 13.2 Process

1. **Report or observation:** the matter is logged with HR.
2. **Preliminary review:** HR decides whether an investigation is needed (within 5 working days).
3. **Investigation:** an investigator who is not in the employee's reporting line gathers facts and interviews the employee and witnesses. The employee is told the allegations in writing and may respond (**right to be heard**). For gross misconduct, the employee may be **suspended with pay** during the investigation.
4. **Decision:** made by the department head and HRBP, proportionate to the facts, with the employee's record and any mitigating factors considered.
5. **Communication:** written outcome to the employee within 5 working days of the decision.
6. **Appeal:** the employee can appeal in writing to the CHRO within **7 days**. The appeal is decided within 15 working days by someone not involved in the original decision.

Warnings remain on record for **12 months** (verbal) or **24 months** (written).

### 13.3 Performance is separate

Under-performance is handled through the **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** process (60 days, with clear goals and fortnightly check-ins), not through the disciplinary process, unless it involves misconduct.

## 14. Scenarios

**Scenario 1:** A support agent's cousin asks the agent to "just add ₹200 CraveCart Money" for a late order.
*Answer:* Not allowed. The cousin must contact support like any customer, and another agent will handle it under policy. Issuing credit to a relative is gross misconduct.

**Scenario 2:** A product manager wants to paste last week's customer complaint exports into a public AI chatbot to summarise them.
*Answer:* Not allowed. Customer data may only go into approved, company-licensed tools. Use the internal AI workspace, which is approved for confidential data.

**Scenario 3:** A restaurant owner offers a city manager a free family dinner for Diwali.
*Answer:* Decline politely. A box of sweets under ₹2,000 is acceptable; a free meal for the family is not.

**Scenario 4:** An engineer notices a colleague regularly downloading customer phone numbers to a personal laptop.
*Answer:* Report it to `security@cravecart.example` or the Ethics Hotline. Reporting in good faith is protected.

**Scenario 5:** A team lead publicly mocks an agent's English during a team call.
*Answer:* This is disrespectful behaviour (a major violation). The agent, or any witness, can raise it with the HRBP.
