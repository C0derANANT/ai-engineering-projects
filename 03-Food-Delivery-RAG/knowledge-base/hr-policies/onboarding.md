# Employee Onboarding Policy and Guide

_Effective: 1 April 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: People Team (Talent Acquisition and People Operations) · Policy ID: HR-POL-002_

## 1. Purpose

A good start makes people productive and happy sooner. This document explains what happens between accepting an offer and being confirmed after probation, who is responsible for each step, and how edge cases are handled. It applies to all full-time employees and interns in India.

## 2. Onboarding at a glance

| Phase | When | Owner | Key outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-joining | Offer acceptance → Day 0 | Talent Acquisition, People Ops, IT | Documents, background verification, laptop, accounts |
| Day 1 | First day | People Ops, manager, buddy | Welcome, induction, tools working, first team meeting |
| Week 1 | Days 1–5 | Manager, buddy | Role context, 30-60-90 plan agreed, mandatory trainings started |
| First 30 days | — | Manager | Mandatory trainings done, first small deliverable, customer immersion |
| 60 and 90 days | — | Manager, HRBP | Ownership of a scope area, 90-day check-in |
| Probation review | Months 3 and 6 | Manager, HRBP | Confirmation, extension or separation |

## 3. Pre-joining

### 3.1 Offer and acceptance

- Offers are sent through the recruitment system and must be accepted within **5 working days**, otherwise they lapse.
- The offer letter states the role, grade, compensation (fixed, variable, ESOPs if applicable), work location, joining date, probation period and notice period.
- After acceptance, the candidate receives a **CravePeople pre-joining account** to upload documents.

### 3.2 Documents required (upload at least 7 days before joining)

1. PAN card
2. Aadhaar card (masked copy, with the first 8 digits hidden, is acceptable)
3. Passport (if available, required for international travel roles)
4. Educational certificates (highest degree, plus Class 10 and 12 mark sheets)
5. Relieving letters and the last 3 payslips from the previous employer (or a resignation acceptance if the relieving letter is pending)
6. Bank account details (a cancelled cheque or bank statement)
7. Universal Account Number (UAN) for Provident Fund, if previously employed
8. Passport-size photograph
9. Emergency contact details
10. Nomination forms for PF, gratuity and insurance (can be completed in week 1)

### 3.3 Background verification (BGV)

- Carried out by an authorised third-party agency with the candidate's written consent.
- **Checks:** identity, address, education (highest degree), employment (last two employers), criminal record database check and, for finance and leadership roles, a credit check.
- **Timeline:** usually **7–10 working days**. Joining can go ahead while BGV is in progress, but access to sensitive production systems and customer data is granted **only after BGV is clear**.
- **Discrepancies:** the candidate is told and given **5 working days** to explain or provide documents. Material misrepresentation (fake degrees, undisclosed termination, wrong dates hiding employment at a competitor) can lead to the offer being withdrawn, or to termination if the person has already joined.

### 3.4 IT and workspace setup

- **Laptop:** a MacBook or Windows laptop, depending on role. Delivered to the office desk or **couriered to the home address 2 working days before joining** for remote joiners.
- **Accounts:** company email, Slack, CravePeople, VPN, the role-specific tools (for example GitHub, Jira, Agent Console or Restaurant Partner Hub admin), and single sign-on with multi-factor authentication.
- **Access card** for offices, issued on day 1.
- **Welcome kit:** a CraveCart hoodie, notebook, water bottle and a ₹500 CraveCart Money welcome credit.

### 3.5 Buddy assignment

Every new joiner gets a **buddy**: a peer from the same or a nearby team, at least 6 months at CraveCart, and not the new joiner's manager. The buddy is introduced by email 3 days before joining.

## 4. Day 1

Typical Day 1 in a CraveCart office (remote joiners follow the same schedule on video):

| Time (IST) | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:30 am | Welcome at reception; access card, laptop pickup (if not shipped) |
| 10:00 am | **People induction** (People Ops): company story, values, org structure, CravePeople tour, payroll and benefits enrolment |
| 11:30 am | **IT setup:** log in, set up MFA, VPN, Slack and email; security basics |
| 12:30 pm | Meet the manager: role expectations, team introductions |
| 1:00 pm | Team lunch, ordered on CraveCart, of course, using the meal benefit |
| 2:30 pm | Meet the buddy: office tour, team rituals, "who to ask for what" |
| 3:30 pm | Start mandatory trainings (section 6) |
| 5:00 pm | End-of-day check-in with the manager |

**Manager's Day 1 checklist:** calendar invites for team meetings sent; first-week schedule shared; a starter task identified; introductions to 5 key stakeholders planned.

## 5. Week 1

- **Day 2:** role-specific deep dive. For example, engineers set up the development environment and ship a "hello world" change to staging; support agents start classroom training; city managers meet their restaurant account managers.
- **Day 3:** product walkthrough. Place a real order, track it, and raise a test support ticket in the sandbox environment.
- **Day 4:** meet cross-functional partners (product, operations, customer experience).
- **Day 5:** agree the **30-60-90 day plan** with the manager and record it in CravePeople.

## 6. Mandatory trainings

| Training | Deadline | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code of Conduct (read and acknowledge) | 14 days | 45 min | Annual re-acknowledgement in April |
| POSH awareness | 14 days | 60 min | Annual refresher |
| Information Security and Data Privacy (DPDP Act) | 14 days | 60 min | Must be passed (80% or more) before getting customer-data access |
| Anti-bribery and conflicts of interest | 30 days | 30 min | — |
| Food safety basics | 30 days | 30 min | Restaurant Operations, CX and Quality roles only |
| Field safety | Before the first ride-along | 20 min | All employees doing field visits |

Overdue mandatory training is escalated to the manager after 7 days and to the department head after 14 days. Access to sensitive systems may be paused until it is completed.

## 7. Customer immersion ("Know the Kitchen")

Within their **first 60 days**, every corporate employee (all grades, including leadership hires) completes:

1. **Half a day shadowing customer support**: listening to live chats with a senior agent.
2. **One ride-along with a delivery partner** during a lunch or dinner peak (pillion, helmet provided, with the partner's consent). Alternatively, a half-day at a dispatch hub for employees who can't ride.
3. **One restaurant visit** with a Restaurant Operations associate, to see how orders arrive and are packed.

New joiners share three insights from immersion with their team. Many product improvements have started this way.

## 8. The 30-60-90 day plan

| By day | Typical goals |
|---|---|
| 30 | Understands the team's metrics and systems; mandatory trainings complete; immersion started; first small deliverable shipped |
| 60 | Owns a defined scope (a feature, a city zone, a support queue); immersion complete; working independently on routine tasks |
| 90 | Delivers results in the owned scope; gives and receives feedback; has a development goal for the next quarter |

The manager and new joiner hold a **formal check-in at day 30 and day 90**, documented in CravePeople. HR sends an anonymous **onboarding survey** at day 30 and day 90.

## 9. Probation

- **Probation period:** 6 months for all full-time employees (3 months for interns converting to full-time).
- **Reviews:** at month 3 (mid-probation feedback) and month 6 (confirmation decision).
- **Outcomes at month 6:**
  - **Confirmed:** a confirmation letter is issued through CravePeople, and the notice period changes from 30 to **60 days** (90 days for Grade L7 and above).
  - **Extended:** once, by up to **3 months**, with written reasons and clear goals.
  - **Not confirmed:** employment ends with 30 days' notice or pay in lieu.
- If no decision is recorded within **15 days** after probation ends, the employee is **deemed confirmed**.
- Leave during probation follows `hr-policies/leave-policy.md` (EL can be used after 3 months).

## 10. Edge cases

**Joining date change:** a candidate may ask to move the joining date up to **2 times**, by up to 30 days in total, with the hiring manager's agreement. Longer delays need Talent Acquisition head approval and may lead to the offer being reconsidered.

**Previous employer hasn't released the candidate:** the joining date can be moved. If the candidate has to serve a longer notice, Talent Acquisition may, in exceptional cases, support a **notice buyout** of up to 30 days of fixed pay, recovered if the employee leaves within 12 months.

**Relocation:** employees relocating for the role at CraveCart's request receive:
- a **₹50,000** relocation allowance (₹75,000 for Grade L6 and above),
- **up to 14 nights** of company-paid temporary accommodation,
- travel for the employee and immediate family.
The allowance is recovered pro-rata if the employee resigns within 12 months.

**Remote joiners:** the laptop is couriered, induction is held on video, and the access card is issued on the first office visit. Remote joiners in a city with a CraveCart office should visit within the first 30 days.

**Rehires (boomerang employees):** former employees who left in good standing within 24 months may rejoin with a shortened onboarding. BGV covers only the gap period. Previous service counts towards gratuity only as provided under the Payment of Gratuity Act.

**Interns:** onboarding is shortened (Day 1, mandatory trainings, buddy). Interns get read-only access to production data unless their manager justifies more access and BGV is clear.

**BGV pending past 30 days:** People Ops follows up with the agency weekly. The employee continues working with restricted access. Probation confirmation can't be completed until BGV is clear.

## 11. Responsibilities summary

| Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **New joiner** | Upload documents on time; complete trainings; agree the 30-60-90 plan; ask questions |
| **Manager** | Prepare the first week; set clear expectations; hold day 30 and day 90 check-ins; make the probation decision on time |
| **Buddy** | Meet at least weekly for the first 4 weeks; answer "how things work here" questions; invite to social events |
| **People Ops** | Documents, BGV, induction, payroll and benefits enrolment, confirmation letters |
| **IT** | Laptop, accounts, access by role, security setup |
| **HRBP** | Escalations, probation extensions, onboarding survey follow-up |

## 12. Frequently asked questions

**Q: When will I get my first salary?**
A: Payroll runs on the last working day of each month. If you join after the 20th, your first salary for that month's days is paid with the next month's salary.

**Q: When does my health insurance start?**
A: From your joining date. Add dependants in CravePeople within **30 days** of joining. See `hr-policies/benefits.md`.

**Q: I don't have my relieving letter yet. Can I still join?**
A: Yes. Upload your resignation acceptance email, and provide the relieving letter within 60 days.

**Q: Can I skip the delivery ride-along?**
A: You can do a dispatch hub half-day instead, but customer immersion itself is mandatory.
