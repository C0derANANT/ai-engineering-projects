# CraveCart Cancellation Policy

_Effective: 1 October 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Customer Experience and Restaurant Operations_

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out when a customer, a restaurant or CraveCart can cancel an order, what fees apply, and the exceptions. Step-by-step instructions are in `ordering/cancelling-an-order.md`. Refund timelines are in `policies/refund-policy.md`.

## 2. Why cancellation fees exist

Once a restaurant starts cooking, the food usually can't be resold, and a delivery partner may already be on the way. Cancellation fees pay the restaurant and the partner for work already done. They are **not** a source of profit for CraveCart: the fee goes to the restaurant (for food) and to the delivery partner (for a trip already started).

## 3. Customer-initiated cancellations

### 3.1 Fee schedule

| Stage when the customer cancels | Cancellation fee | Customer gets back |
|---|---|---|
| **A.** Within 60 seconds of placing the order (grace period), at any status | ₹0 | 100% of amount paid |
| **B.** After 60 seconds, but before the restaurant accepts | ₹0 | 100% of amount paid |
| **C.** Restaurant has accepted; status is "Order confirmed" (not yet preparing) | **50%** of (item total + packaging), after discounts | Amount paid minus fee |
| **D.** Status is "Preparing" or later (including "Picked up") | **100%** of amount paid | ₹0 (except tip, if not yet picked up) |
| **E.** Refused at the door (COD or prepaid) | **100%** of order value | ₹0 |

Notes:

- The stage is decided at the moment the customer **confirms** the cancellation, not when they open the Help screen.
- The **platform fee and GST** on fees are kept in stage C and D cancellations. The **delivery fee** is refunded in stage C if no delivery partner has reached the restaurant yet.
- The **tip** is refunded in stages A, B and C, and in stage D if the order has not been picked up.

### 3.2 Worked examples

**Example 1: stage C**
Item total ₹500, packaging ₹20, coupon −₹50, delivery fee ₹28, platform fee ₹6, GST ₹23.50 (food) + ₹6.12 (fees), tip ₹20. Total paid ₹553.62.
Fee = 50% × (₹500 + ₹20 − ₹50) = 50% × ₹470 = ₹235, plus GST on that portion at 5% = ₹11.75. Platform fee and its GST (₹6 + ₹1.08) are kept.
Customer gets back approximately ₹553.62 − ₹235 − ₹11.75 − ₹7.08 = **₹299.79**. The coupon is not restored.

**Example 2: stage D**
Same order, cancelled while "Preparing", before pickup. The customer gets back only the **₹20 tip**.

**Example 3: grace period**
The customer orders at 9:00:00 pm and cancels at 9:00:45 pm. The restaurant had already accepted at 9:00:20. The cancellation is still free because it is within 60 seconds. Full refund, and the coupon is restored.

## 4. Exceptions: free cancellation at any stage

A customer can cancel with **no fee and a full refund** (including fees and tip) at **any** stage if:

1. **Very late:** the order hasn't been delivered **30 minutes after the promised delivery time** shown at checkout.
2. **No delivery partner:** no partner has been assigned **20 minutes after the food was marked ready**.
3. **Price or item discrepancy:** the restaurant changed the order without the customer's consent, for example by substituting an item.
4. **Safety concern:** the customer reports a safety issue with the assigned delivery partner, verified by Trust & Safety.
5. **Wrong ETA from a CraveCart error:** a technical problem made the ETA at checkout clearly wrong (for example, a restaurant 18 km away shown as 25 minutes).
6. **Documented emergency:** a medical or similar emergency, approved by a team lead. This is limited to once per 12 months per account.

**Goodwill waiver:** agents may waive a stage C fee once per 12 months for accounts older than 6 months with no prior waiver and no abuse flags.

## 5. Scheduled Orders

| When cancelled | Fee |
|---|---|
| More than 60 minutes before the slot starts | ₹0, full refund |
| Within 60 minutes of the slot, restaurant not yet preparing | Stage C fee (50%) |
| Restaurant preparing, or later | Stage D fee (100%) |

If CraveCart or the restaurant can't fulfil a Scheduled Order, it is cancelled with a full refund, and the customer is told at least 30 minutes before the slot where possible.

## 6. Cash on Delivery orders

- Fees are calculated the same way.
- Since nothing was paid upfront, the fee becomes **outstanding dues** on the account. Outstanding dues must be paid before or with the next order, and the next order must be paid online.
- **Two** COD orders refused or not paid at the door within **90 days** disable COD on the account for **180 days**.
- Outstanding dues over ₹1,500 block new orders until paid.

## 7. Restaurant-initiated cancellations

Restaurants may reject or cancel an order only for these reasons:

1. Item(s) out of stock
2. Kitchen closing or closed
3. Too busy to fulfil within a reasonable time
4. Unable to meet a special instruction that is essential to the order
5. Suspected fraudulent order, flagged to CraveCart

**Customer impact:** always a **full refund**, automatically, with an explanation in the app. If the restaurant cancels after **10 minutes or more** of the order being accepted, the customer may receive an apology coupon (usually ₹75 off, valid 7 days).

**Restaurant consequences (internal):**

- Each cancellation after acceptance is recorded as a **reliability strike**.
- 3 strikes in 7 days: the restaurant is ranked lower in search for 7 days.
- 6 strikes in 30 days: listing paused for 48 hours and a mandatory call with the Restaurant Operations account manager.
- Repeated stock-out cancellations: the restaurant must update its menu availability, and items may be auto-disabled.

## 8. CraveCart-initiated cancellations

CraveCart may cancel an order, with a full refund, when:

- service in the area is paused (severe weather, floods, curfews, civil unrest, government orders),
- no delivery partner can be found within a reasonable time,
- the assigned partner has an accident or emergency and no replacement can complete the order,
- the order is flagged as fraudulent (for example, stolen payment instruments or fake COD orders), in which case the refund goes only to the original source,
- a technical failure has made the order impossible to fulfil correctly.

## 9. Excessive cancellations

To protect restaurants and delivery partners from wasted work:

- Customers who make **more than 3 paid-stage (C, D or E) cancellations in 30 days** have COD disabled, and their account is flagged for review.
- Patterns suggesting abuse (for example, repeatedly ordering and cancelling at the door) can lead to account restrictions under the Terms of Use.

## 10. Plus membership cancellation

- Cancel within **7 days** of purchase with **no benefit used** for a full refund.
- Otherwise the membership remains active until the end of the paid period, with no refund.
- Turning off auto-renew is not a cancellation. The membership continues until expiry and simply does not renew.

## 11. Disputes

- Customers who disagree with a cancellation fee can raise a dispute from Help > select order > "Dispute cancellation fee" within **7 days**.
- A human agent reviews the order timeline (restaurant acceptance time, preparation start, partner location) and responds within **24 hours**.
- If unresolved, the customer can escalate to the Grievance Officer (`grievance@cravecart.example`), who acknowledges within 48 hours and resolves within one month.

## 12. Quick reference for agents

- **Check the timestamp of cancellation confirmation** against the restaurant's "accepted" and "preparing" timestamps in the order timeline.
- **Grace period** = order created time + 60 seconds (server time).
- **Promised time** = order created time + upper ETA at checkout (stored as `promised_delivery_at`).
- **Late-free-cancel eligible** if now > `promised_delivery_at` + 30 minutes.
- **No-partner-free-cancel eligible** if now > `food_ready_at` + 20 minutes and `partner_assigned_at` is null.
- Never promise a waiver before checking eligibility. Explain the fee in plain words and show the breakdown.
