# CraveCart Refund Policy

_Effective: 1 October 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Customer Experience and Finance · Applies to: all customer orders placed in India_

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains when customers get money back, how much, how it is paid, and how long it takes. It applies to food orders, CraveCart Plus memberships, tips and wallet balances. Cancellation fees are covered in `policies/cancellation-policy.md`. This policy covers refunds that follow a cancellation and refunds for problems with delivered orders.

Nothing in this policy limits a customer's rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

## 2. Principles

1. **Customers should not pay for what they didn't get.** If an item is missing, wrong or unfit to eat, the customer is refunded for it.
2. **Refunds reflect what was actually paid.** Discounts, coupons and CraveCart Money are accounted for.
3. **Fast by default.** Clear-cut cases are refunded automatically or by Crave Assist in minutes. Only unclear or high-value cases go to a human reviewer.
4. **Fair to restaurants and delivery partners.** Refunds caused by a restaurant's error are recovered from the restaurant. Partners are never charged for food quality problems.

## 3. Refund eligibility at a glance

| Situation | Refund | Reporting window | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order cancelled in grace period / before acceptance | Full | — (automatic) | None |
| Order cancelled by restaurant or CraveCart | Full | — (automatic) | None |
| Order cancelled more than 30 min after promised time | Full | — (automatic) | None |
| Order cancelled by customer after acceptance | Order value minus cancellation fee | — (automatic) | None |
| Missing item(s) | Item value | 3 hours after delivery | Photo of the bill or receipt and the items received, if asked |
| Wrong item(s) | Item value, or replacement | 3 hours after delivery | Photo of the wrong item |
| Spilled or damaged packaging | Value of affected items (full if whole order unusable) | 3 hours after delivery | Photo of the spill or damage |
| Quality issue (stale, spoiled, undercooked, burnt) | Value of affected items | 3 hours after delivery | Photo; description |
| Foreign object, hygiene issue or suspected food poisoning | Full order, plus food safety investigation | 24 hours after delivery (illness: 48 hours) | Photo, and medical documents if claiming expenses |
| Quantity less than described (for example, 6 pieces instead of 8) | Proportional item value | 3 hours after delivery | Photo |
| Marked "Delivered" but not received | Full (after verification) | 24 hours after delivery | None up front; agent verifies |
| Charged more than the checkout amount, or a fee charged wrongly | Difference | 30 days | Order ID |
| Double charge for one order | Extra charge | 30 days | Bank statement or UPI reference |
| Money debited but no order created | Full (automatic bank reversal) | 30 days | UPI or bank reference if not reversed in 7 business days |
| Plus membership, within 7 days, no benefit used | Full membership fee | 7 days from purchase | None |

## 4. How to request a refund

1. Go to **Help** > select the order > choose the issue (for example, "Items missing", "Wrong item received", "Food quality issue").
2. Select the affected items and quantities.
3. Upload photos if asked. Photos must be taken **after delivery** and show the item or packaging clearly. Photos from the internet or of a different order will be rejected.
4. Crave Assist decides instantly for most cases. Others go to a human agent, who responds within the SLA in `support/customer-support-procedures.md`.

Requests can also be made through email or the grievance officer, but in-app reporting is fastest.

## 5. How the refund amount is calculated

### 5.1 Item-level refunds

**Refund = item price + item's share of packaging + GST on both − item's share of any discount**

- **Packaging share:** if packaging is charged per item, that item's packaging. If charged per order, it is split in proportion to item prices.
- **Discount share:** coupons, Plus discounts and restaurant offers are spread across all items in proportion to their price. The refund is reduced by the share that applied to the affected item.
- **Delivery, platform, small cart, high-demand and late-night fees** are refunded only if the **whole order** is refunded.

**Worked example**

Item total ₹800: Chicken Biryani ₹350, Mutton Korma ₹300, Raita ₹50, Gulab Jamun ₹100. Per-order packaging ₹40. Coupon: 20% off up to ₹120 (gives ₹120 off).

The Mutton Korma (₹300) was missing.

- Item share of order = 300 / 800 = 37.5%
- Packaging share = 37.5% × ₹40 = ₹15
- Discount share = 37.5% × ₹120 = ₹45
- Pre-tax refund = ₹300 + ₹15 − ₹45 = ₹270
- GST (5%) on ₹270 = ₹13.50
- **Refund = ₹283.50**

### 5.2 Full-order refunds

The full amount paid is refunded, including fees and tip, through the original payment split (for example, ₹100 to CraveCart Money and ₹450 to UPI). The coupon is restored if it is still valid.

### 5.3 Replacement instead of refund

- For missing or wrong items, customers can sometimes choose **"Send the missing item"**. This is offered when the restaurant is open and willing, the report is made within **45 minutes** of delivery, and the restaurant is within 7 km.
- Replacement delivery is free. If the replacement fails, a full refund for that item is given.

## 6. Refund methods and timelines

| Original payment method | Default refund destination | Time to reach the customer |
|---|---|---|
| CraveCart Money | CraveCart Money | Instant (within 10 minutes) |
| UPI | Source UPI account | 1–3 business days |
| Debit card | Source card | 5–7 business days |
| Credit card | Source card | 5–7 business days (may show in the next statement) |
| Net banking | Source bank account | 5–7 business days |
| Third-party wallet | Source wallet | 1–3 business days |
| Pay Later | Pay Later provider | 3–5 business days |
| Cash on Delivery | CraveCart Money (default) or the customer's UPI ID / bank account on request | Instant to CraveCart Money; 3–5 business days to UPI or bank |

- **Instant refund option:** for any payment method, customers can choose to receive the refund in **CraveCart Money instantly** instead of to the source.
- **Refund reference:** for card, UPI and net banking refunds, a bank reference number (ARN/RRN) appears in Orders > select order > "Refund status" within 48 hours. Customers can give this to their bank if the money hasn't arrived.
- **Delays beyond the stated time:** if a refund hasn't arrived 2 business days after the maximum time above, contact support with the reference number. The Payments team investigates with the payment gateway within 3 business days.

## 7. CraveCart Money refunds

- Refunds credited to CraveCart Money **do not expire**.
- Refund balances can be moved back to the original payment source within **30 days** of crediting, through support. After 30 days they stay in CraveCart Money.
- Promotional CraveCart Money (cashback, apology credits, On-Time Promise credits) **cannot** be withdrawn and has an expiry date shown in the wallet.
- The maximum wallet balance is ₹10,000. If a refund would exceed it, the excess goes to the source payment method.

## 8. What is not refundable

1. **Taste or preference complaints** where the food matches its description (for example, "too spicy" for a dish marked spicy, or "didn't like the flavour").
2. **Reports made after the window** in section 3, except where the law requires otherwise or a team lead approves an exception.
3. **Orders not delivered because of the customer:** wrong address that was not corrected, customer unreachable after the 7-minute wait and two calls, or refusing a correct order at the door.
4. **Cancellation fees** charged correctly under the cancellation policy.
5. **Tips**, once the order has been delivered.
6. **Plus membership fees** after 7 days or after any benefit has been used.
7. **Claims without required evidence**, when evidence was asked for and not provided within 24 hours.
8. **Items eaten in full** before a quality complaint, except for foreign-object or food-safety cases.

## 9. Food safety cases

- **Foreign objects** (hair, plastic, metal, insects), signs of tampering with the seal, or suspected food poisoning are treated as **P1 food safety incidents**.
- The customer receives a **full order refund** as soon as a photo is provided.
- The **Food Safety team** contacts the customer within **24 hours**. The restaurant is investigated, and its listing may be suspended pending an audit.
- **Medical expenses:** where a customer reports illness within 48 hours and provides a doctor's prescription or hospital bill, CraveCart may reimburse documented, reasonable medical costs **up to ₹10,000** after investigation. This is goodwill, not an admission of liability.
- If the seal is broken on delivery, customers should **not accept the order**. Report it immediately and it will be refunded in full.

## 10. Refund abuse and fraud controls

CraveCart reviews accounts that show unusual refund patterns. To keep things fair:

- Accounts with **more than 4 refund claims in 30 days**, or claims worth **more than ₹3,000 in 30 days**, go to **manual review**. Instant refunds pause for that account until review is complete (within 48 hours).
- Reused or edited photos, photos from other orders, and false "not received" claims contradicted by the delivery PIN or photo proof lead to a **warning**, then **refund restrictions**, and then **account suspension**.
- Accounts suspended for fraud lose Plus benefits and promotional CraveCart Money. Refund balances in CraveCart Money remain withdrawable to the source.
- A customer can appeal a decision by emailing the Grievance Officer. Appeals are decided within 15 days.

## 11. Edge cases

**Order partly paid with a coupon and partly with CraveCart Money; one item missing.**
The item-level refund (section 5.1) is credited first to CraveCart Money up to the amount paid from it, and the rest to the other payment method.

**Customer reports a missing item; the restaurant says it was packed.**
If the bag's seal was intact and the restaurant confirms with a packing photo, the claim goes to a human agent. When the evidence is unclear, the policy favours the customer for the **first two** such claims in 90 days.

**Restaurant sent a more expensive item instead of the one ordered.**
Customers may keep it at no charge. If the customer wanted the original item, a refund for the ordered item is given.

**Wrong item that conflicts with dietary needs (non-veg sent instead of veg).**
A full refund for the item **plus** the whole order if the customer says the rest of the order was contaminated. Escalated to the Food Safety team as a P1. The restaurant receives an automatic quality strike.

**Customer was charged, the order was cancelled, and the refund shows "processed" but isn't in the bank.**
Share the ARN/RRN. Banks may take the full time in section 6. After that, the Payments team raises a chargeback inquiry with the gateway.

**Group Cart order with a missing item belonging to a participant.**
Only the host can raise the claim. The refund goes to the host's payment method.

**CraveCart for Business orders.**
Refunds go back to the company's meal wallet or invoice, not to the employee's personal account.

## 12. Restaurant and delivery partner accountability (internal)

- Refunds caused by the restaurant (missing, wrong, quality or quantity issues) are **deducted from the restaurant's next weekly payout**, with the evidence shown in the Restaurant Partner Hub. Restaurants can dispute within 7 days.
- Refunds caused by spillage in transit are absorbed by CraveCart unless the delivery partner was clearly negligent, such as failing to use the insulated bag. In that case the partner receives coaching, **not** a financial penalty.
- Refunds caused by CraveCart (technical errors, wrong fees) are absorbed by CraveCart.
