# CraveCart: Company Overview

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Corporate Communications_

## 1. Who we are

CraveCart Technologies Private Limited ("CraveCart") is an Indian online food ordering and delivery platform. Customers use the CraveCart app (Android and iOS) or the CraveCart website to discover restaurants, order food and have it delivered by an independent delivery partner, usually in 25 to 40 minutes.

- **Founded:** March 2016 in Bengaluru, Karnataka
- **Founders:** Rohan Mehta (Chief Executive Officer) and Ishita Rao (Chief Operating Officer)
- **Headquarters:** Indiranagar, Bengaluru
- **Regional offices:** Gurugram, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune and Kolkata
- **Cities served:** 46 (as of November 2026)
- **Restaurant partners:** more than 1,10,000 active restaurants and cloud kitchens
- **Delivery partners:** about 2,40,000 active delivery partners each month
- **Full-time employees:** about 5,200
- **Orders:** about 1.1 million orders per day on average in 2026

## 2. Mission and values

**Mission:** "Good food, delivered reliably, at a fair price."

**Core values**

1. **Customer First:** every decision starts with what is right for the customer, the restaurant and the delivery partner.
2. **Own It:** we take responsibility for outcomes, not just tasks.
3. **Move Fast, Safely:** we ship quickly but never compromise on food safety, road safety or data privacy.
4. **Respect Everyone:** customers, restaurant partners, delivery partners and colleagues are treated with dignity.
5. **Be Honest:** we tell customers the real delivery time, the real fees and the real reason when something goes wrong.

## 3. Products and services

| Product | What it is |

CraveCart does **not** sell or deliver alcohol, tobacco or medicines.

## 4. How an order works

1. The customer places an order and pays (or chooses Cash on Delivery).
2. The restaurant must accept the order within **3 minutes**. If it does not, the order is cancelled automatically with a full refund.
3. The restaurant prepares the food. CraveCart assigns the nearest suitable delivery partner so that they arrive as the food is ready.
4. The delivery partner picks up the sealed package and travels to the customer. The customer can track the trip live on a map.
5. The order is delivered and the customer can rate the food and the delivery separately.

## 5. Who CraveCart works with

- **Customers:** anyone aged 18 or over with a verified Indian mobile number. Accounts are personal and may not be shared or resold.
- **Restaurant partners:** independent businesses that list their menus on CraveCart. Restaurants set their own menu prices and packaging charges and are responsible for food quality and hygiene. Every partner must hold a valid FSSAI licence.
- **Delivery partners:** independent contractors (not CraveCart employees). They are paid per trip, plus incentives and 100% of customer tips. CraveCart provides accident insurance and a 24x7 emergency helpline for them.
- **Employees:** full-time staff in technology, operations, customer support, sales, finance, HR and other functions. The internal HR policies in the `hr-policies` folder apply only to employees.

Service availability is decided at the level of the delivery address, not the city. Some outer areas of a listed city may be unserviceable. The app shows "We don't deliver here yet" (error code `CC-LOC-201`) when an address is out of range.

## 9. Operating hours

- Most cities: **6:00 am to 2:00 am** (each restaurant sets its own hours within this window).
- Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad: **24 hours** in selected zones, with a late-night fee from 11:00 pm to 5:59 am.
- Service can be paused in an area during extreme weather, floods, curfews or civil unrest. Orders already placed in a paused area are cancelled with a full refund.
