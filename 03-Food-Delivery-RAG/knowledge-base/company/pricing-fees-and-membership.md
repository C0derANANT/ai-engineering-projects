# CraveCart Pricing, Fees and Plus Membership

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Applies to: all customer orders in India · Owner: Pricing and Monetisation team_

This document explains every charge a customer can see on a CraveCart bill, how each is calculated, and how CraveCart Plus membership changes them. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹).

## 1. Anatomy of a bill

Example order from a restaurant 5.2 km away, placed at 8:15 pm on a rainy evening by a customer **without** Plus:

| Line | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Item total | ₹480.00 | 2 × Paneer Butter Masala (₹200), 1 × Butter Naan pack (₹80). Prices set by the restaurant. |
| Packaging charges | ₹30.00 | Set by the restaurant. |
| Delivery fee | ₹37.60 | ₹20 for the first 3 km + ₹8 × 2.2 km. |
| High-demand (rain) fee | ₹20.00 | Applied during heavy rain. |
| Platform fee | ₹6.00 | Flat, per order. |
| Coupon `TASTY60` | −₹60.00 | Restaurant-funded coupon. |
| GST on food and packaging (5%) | ₹22.50 | 5% of (₹480 + ₹30 − ₹60 coupon). |
| GST on fees (18%) | ₹11.45 | 18% of (₹37.60 + ₹20 + ₹6). |
| Delivery partner tip | ₹20.00 | Optional; 100% goes to the delivery partner. |
| **To pay** | **₹567.55** | |

The bill breakdown is always visible on the checkout screen (tap "Bill details") and in Orders > select order > "View bill".

## 2. Item prices and packaging charges

- Menu prices are set by the restaurant and may differ from dine-in prices. CraveCart shows the price the restaurant gives us.
- Packaging charges are set by the restaurant, per item or per order, and appear as a separate line before payment.
- If a restaurant's price on the bill differs from the price shown on the menu at the time of ordering, the customer is entitled to a refund of the difference. See `policies/refund-policy.md`.

## 3. Delivery fee

The delivery fee depends on the road distance between the restaurant and the delivery address.

| Distance | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 3 km | ₹20 |
| Each additional km (charged per 100 m) | ₹8 per km |
| Maximum delivery fee | ₹70 |

- **Maximum delivery distance:** 10 km in most cities; 7 km in dense zones of Mumbai, Bengaluru and Delhi NCR.
- **Free-delivery restaurants:** some restaurants pay the delivery fee themselves. These show a "Free delivery" tag.
- **CraveCart Plus:** the delivery fee is waived on orders with an item total of ₹199 or more from restaurants within 7 km (see section 8).

**Worked example:** a restaurant 7.5 km away is charged ₹20 + (4.5 × ₹8) = ₹56. A restaurant 9.8 km away would be ₹20 + (6.8 × ₹8) = ₹74.40, but is capped at ₹70.

## 4. Small cart fee

- A **₹25** small cart fee is added when the item total (before discounts) is **below ₹149**.
- It disappears as soon as the item total reaches ₹149.
- It applies to Plus members too, but because Plus free delivery needs ₹199 or more, members rarely see it on free-delivery orders.

## 5. Platform fee

- A flat **₹6 per order** for all customers, including Plus members and CraveCart for Business orders.
- It supports app operations, payment processing and 24x7 support.
- It is refunded only if the whole order is cancelled without a fee or refunded in full.

## 6. High-demand (surge) fee

- **₹10 to ₹40** per order when demand in an area is much higher than the number of available delivery partners: heavy rain, major festivals (Diwali, New Year's Eve, Eid), big cricket matches and similar events.
- The fee and its reason ("Heavy rain in your area") are shown on the checkout screen before payment. It is never added after the order is placed.
- Plus members also pay the high-demand fee, but it is capped at **₹20** for them.

## 7. Late-night fee

- **₹15** for orders placed between **11:00 pm and 5:59 am**.
- It covers extra pay for delivery partners who ride at night.
- It applies only where late-night service exists (see `company/overview.md`, section 9).

## 8. Taxes (GST)

- **5% GST** on food items and packaging, calculated **after** coupon and restaurant discounts. CraveCart collects and pays this as the e-commerce operator under Section 9(5) of the CGST Act.
- **18% GST** on the delivery fee, platform fee, small cart fee, high-demand fee and late-night fee.
- Tips and CraveCart Plus discounts are not taxed. Plus membership fees carry 18% GST, included in the price shown.
- A GST invoice is available in Orders > select order > "Download invoice". Business customers can add their GSTIN in Profile > Business details **before** placing the order. A GSTIN cannot be added to an invoice after the order is placed.

## 9. Tips

- Customers can tip ₹10, ₹20, ₹30 or a custom amount up to ₹500 at checkout, during delivery, or up to **24 hours after delivery**.
- 100% of the tip goes to the delivery partner. CraveCart keeps none of it.
- A tip can be increased at any time but cannot be reduced or removed once the delivery partner has picked up the order.
- If an order is cancelled before pickup, the tip is refunded. If the order is not delivered for reasons not caused by the customer, the tip is refunded.

## 10. CraveCart Plus membership

### 10.1 Plans

| Plan | Price (incl. GST) | Effective monthly cost |
|---|---|---|
| Plus Quarterly | ₹149 for 3 months | about ₹50 per month |
| Plus Annual | ₹449 for 12 months | about ₹37 per month |

Free trials (usually 1 month) are sometimes offered to new or returning customers. A trial converts to a paid plan only if the customer turns on auto-renew.

### 10.2 Benefits

1. **Free delivery:** delivery fee waived on orders with an item total of **₹199 or more** from restaurants within **7 km**. There is no limit on the number of orders.
2. **Extra discounts:** up to 30% off at "Plus partner" restaurants, marked with a Plus badge. This cannot be combined with other restaurant coupons but can be combined with bank offers.
3. **Lower high-demand fee:** capped at ₹20.
4. **Priority support:** callback from a support agent within 10 minutes on request, and priority in chat queues.
5. **On-Time Promise:** if an eligible Plus order arrives **more than 10 minutes after the promised delivery time** (the upper end of the ETA range shown at checkout), the customer receives **₹50 CraveCart Money** automatically within 24 hours. This is capped at **4 credits per calendar month**.
   - Not eligible: Scheduled Orders, Group Cart orders, orders where the customer changed the address after placing the order, orders delayed because the customer was unreachable, and periods when the high-demand fee is active because of severe weather.

### 10.3 What Plus does not include

- The platform fee, packaging charges, small cart fee (where applicable), late-night fee and GST still apply.
- Free delivery does not apply to restaurants more than 7 km away. Those orders are charged the normal delivery fee.

### 10.4 Renewal and cancellation

- Auto-renew is **off by default**. It can be turned on or off in Profile > CraveCart Plus > Manage.
- A membership can be cancelled within **7 days of purchase for a full refund if no Plus benefit has been used**. After a benefit has been used, or after 7 days, the fee is non-refundable and benefits continue until the end of the period.
- A Plus membership is tied to one CraveCart account and cannot be transferred, shared or paused.
- **Edge case:** if a customer buys Plus while a free trial is active, the paid period starts when the trial ends.
- **Edge case:** an account suspended for refund abuse loses Plus benefits with no refund of the remaining period.

## 11. Coupons and offers

- Only **one coupon per order**. Bank offers (instant discounts from card or UPI partners) apply on top of a coupon if the bank's terms allow.
- A coupon's minimum order value is checked against the item total **before** taxes and fees.
- Coupons are reserved when the order is placed. If the order is cancelled without a fee (for example, before the restaurant accepts), the coupon is restored if it is still within its validity period. Otherwise it is used up.
- Common coupon types: new-user coupons (first 3 orders), restaurant coupons, payment-partner coupons, and apology coupons issued by support.

## 12. Price change edge cases

- **Prices changed between adding to cart and checkout:** the app shows "Prices updated" and the customer must confirm the new total.
- **Restaurant changes a price after the order is placed:** the customer pays the price shown at checkout. The restaurant cannot increase it.
- **Wrong fee charged:** for example, a small cart fee on an order of ₹149 or more, or a high-demand fee not shown before payment. Support refunds the wrong amount in full to CraveCart Money or the original payment method.
