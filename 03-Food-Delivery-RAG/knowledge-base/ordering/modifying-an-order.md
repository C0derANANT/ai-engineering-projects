# Modifying an Order After It Is Placed

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Consumer Product team and Customer Experience_

What you can change depends on how far the order has progressed. The general rule: **the earlier you ask, the more you can change.** This document lists exactly what can be changed at each stage, how, and what happens to the bill.

## 1. Order stages, for reference

| Stage | Shown in app as | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Order placed (60-second grace) | First 60 seconds after placing. |
| 1 | Waiting for restaurant | Restaurant has not yet accepted (maximum 3 minutes). |
| 2 | Order confirmed | Restaurant accepted, preparation not yet started. |
| 3 | Preparing | Restaurant is cooking. |
| 4 | Picked up / On the way | Delivery partner has collected the food. |
| 5 | Arriving | Delivery partner is within about 500 m. |
| 6 | Delivered | Order handed over. |

## 3. The 60-second grace period ("Edit order")

For the first **60 seconds** after you place an order, an **Edit order** button appears on the tracking screen. While you are editing, the order is held and not sent to the restaurant.

- You can add or remove items, change quantities and customisations, change the address, change the payment method, add or remove a coupon, and edit instructions.
- If the new total is higher, you pay the difference (or the whole order is re-authorised, depending on the payment method). If it is lower, the difference is refunded to the original payment method.
- If you don't finish editing within **3 minutes** of opening the editor, the edit is discarded and the original order is sent to the restaurant.

## 4. Removing items after the grace period

1. Open Help > select the order > "I want to change items".
2. Crave Assist or an agent asks the restaurant to remove the item.
3. **Before the restaurant accepts (Stage 1):** the item is removed and refunded in full.
4. **After acceptance but before preparation (Stage 2):** removal depends on the restaurant agreeing. If it agrees, the item is refunded. If it declines, you can keep the order or cancel under the cancellation policy.
5. **Once preparing (Stage 3) or later:** items cannot be removed.

**Refund for a removed item** = item price + its share of packaging + the related GST. If a coupon was applied, the refund is reduced in proportion to the discount. If the remaining item total falls below the coupon's minimum order value, the coupon is removed and the refund is adjusted.

**Example:** your item total is ₹600 with a flat ₹100-off coupon (minimum order ₹500). You remove a ₹150 item. The new item total is ₹450, which is below ₹500, so the coupon no longer applies. Refund = ₹150 − ₹100 (lost coupon) = ₹50, plus the adjusted packaging and GST.

## 5. Adding items

Items cannot be added once the grace period is over, because the restaurant's kitchen ticket is already printed. Place a **new order** from the same restaurant. Support can ask the delivery partner to wait for both orders if they are ready within 10 minutes of each other, but this is not guaranteed.

## 6. Changing the delivery address

### 6.1 Before pickup (Stages 1–3)

1. Tracking screen > "Change address" (or Help > "Change delivery address").
2. The new address must be:
   - within **2 km** of the original address,
   - within the restaurant's delivery radius, and
   - serviceable at that moment.
3. The delivery fee is recalculated. You pay any increase. Any decrease is refunded.
4. The ETA is updated.

### 6.2 After pickup (Stages 4–5)

- Changes are possible only within **500 m** of the original address, and only through chat. The delivery partner is asked to agree.
- Larger changes are not possible. You can arrange for someone to receive the order at the original address, or cancel (no refund at this stage, except where the cancellation policy's exceptions apply).

### 6.3 Edge cases

- **Wrong city or very far address:** if you entered an address far from where you are, the order cannot be redirected. Cancel early: cancellation is free in the grace period and before the restaurant accepts.
- **Address change and Plus:** orders where the address was changed after placing are **not** eligible for the Plus On-Time Promise.
- **Pin was wrong but the text address is right:** contact support. An agent can share the correct location with the delivery partner without formally changing the address.

## 7. Delivery instructions and contact details

- Delivery instructions ("Leave at the door", "Call on arrival", "Don't ring the bell") can be edited from the tracking screen until the order reaches **Arriving**.
- "Leave at the door" is not available for orders with a delivery PIN (item total ₹1,000 or more) or for COD orders.
- The receiver's name and number can be updated through support at any stage before delivery.

## 8. Payment changes

- **COD → online:** allowed at any time before delivery with "Pay online now". Recommended if you won't have change.
- **Online → COD:** not allowed after the grace period.
- **Switching between online methods** (for example, card to UPI) is not possible after the grace period. Payment is already captured.
- **Paying the difference after a change** (for example, a higher delivery fee after an address change): a payment request is sent in the app. If you don't pay within 10 minutes, the change is reverted.

## 9. Tips

- Add or increase a tip at any time up to 24 hours after delivery.
- Decreasing or removing a tip is possible only **before** the delivery partner picks up the order.

## 10. Scheduled Orders

- Items, address, slot and instructions can all be changed until **60 minutes before the slot starts** (Orders > Scheduled > Edit).
- A slot can be moved to any available slot within the next 2 days.
- Within 60 minutes of the slot, the normal stage rules above apply.

## 11. Group Cart orders

Only the **host** can edit or cancel a Group Cart order after it is placed. Participants who want changes must ask the host.

## 12. What support agents can and cannot do

- Agents **can** relay removal requests to the restaurant, update addresses within the limits above, update receiver details, and pass messages to the delivery partner.
- Agents **cannot** add items, change prices, apply a coupon after the grace period, or force a restaurant to change an order it is already preparing.
- Agents must never ask for OTPs, card numbers, CVVs or UPI PINs to make a change.
