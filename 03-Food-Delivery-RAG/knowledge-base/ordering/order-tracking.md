# Order Tracking

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Consumer Product team_

## 1. Where to track an order

- The tracking screen opens automatically after you place an order.
- You can return to it from the **orange banner** on the home screen ("Your order is on the way") or from **Profile > Orders > select order**.
- On the website, tracking is at **Orders > Track order**.
- The tracking screen stays available for 2 hours after delivery. After that, the order moves to order history.

## 2. Order statuses

| Status | Meaning | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Order placed** | CraveCart has received the order. The 60-second grace period is running. | Up to 60 s |
| **Waiting for restaurant** | Sent to the restaurant, waiting for acceptance. | Up to 3 min, then auto-cancelled |
| **Order confirmed** | The restaurant accepted the order. | 0–3 min |
| **Preparing** | The kitchen is cooking. A countdown shows estimated ready time. | 8–25 min |
| **Delivery partner assigned** | The partner's name, photo, vehicle and rating appear. Shown alongside Preparing. | — |
| **Partner at restaurant** | The partner has arrived and is waiting for the food. | 0–10 min |
| **Picked up / On the way** | The food is sealed and with the partner. The live map starts. | 8–20 min |
| **Arriving** | The partner is within about 500 m. | 1–3 min |
| **Delivered** | The order was handed over (or left at the door, if you asked). | — |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by you, the restaurant or CraveCart. The reason is shown. | — |
| **Undelivered** | The partner could not hand over the order (for example, customer unreachable). | — |

## 3. Live map

- The live map starts at pickup and shows the partner's location, updated every **5 to 10 seconds**.
- The route line is an estimate. Partners may take a different, faster route.
- The map may freeze briefly in low-network areas (basements, flyovers, tunnels) and then jump forward. This is normal.
- If the partner's location has not updated for **more than 5 minutes**, the app shows "Location temporarily unavailable" and support is alerted automatically.

## 4. Notifications

You receive push notifications (and SMS if push is off) when:

1. the restaurant accepts the order,
2. a delivery partner is assigned,
3. the order is picked up,
4. the partner is arriving,
5. the order is delivered,
6. there is a delay of more than 10 minutes beyond the promised time,
7. the order is cancelled or a refund is started.

You can turn off promotional notifications without turning off order updates: Profile > Settings > Notifications.

## 5. Contacting the delivery partner

- Tap **Call** on the tracking screen. Calls go through a **masked number**: neither side sees the other's real phone number.
- Masked calling is available from partner assignment until **30 minutes after delivery** (for example, to return a forgotten item).
- In-app chat with the partner offers quick replies ("Please come to Gate 2", "Call me when you arrive") and free text.
- Partners are told not to text while riding. Expect replies when they stop.
- Customers must not ask partners for their personal number, and partners must not ask for yours.

## 6. Delivery PIN

- Orders with an item total of **₹1,000 or more** get a 4-digit **delivery PIN**, shown on the tracking screen and sent by SMS.
- Give the PIN to the partner **only when you have the order in hand**. The partner enters it to complete delivery.
- If you are ordering for someone else, share the PIN with them.
- If the PIN doesn't work (for example, the partner's app is offline), the partner can complete delivery with a photo of the handover, which support reviews.
- **Never share the PIN with anyone on a phone call claiming to be CraveCart.** CraveCart support never asks for the delivery PIN.

## 7. Sharing tracking

- Tap **Share tracking** to send a live link to anyone (no login needed). It shows the status and map but not the bill or your phone number.
- The link stops working 30 minutes after delivery.

## 8. What happens at the door

1. The partner arrives and calls or rings, following your delivery instructions.
2. **Contactless delivery:** if you chose "Leave at the door", the partner leaves the package, takes a photo, and marks it delivered. This is not available for COD orders or orders with a delivery PIN.
3. **If you don't respond:** the partner waits **7 minutes** at the location and tries calling at least **twice**. Support also tries to reach you. If you still can't be reached, the order is marked **Undelivered – customer unavailable**:
   - prepaid orders are not refunded (see `policies/refund-policy.md`),
   - COD orders count as refused and add outstanding dues equal to the order value.
4. **Gated communities:** if security won't let the partner in, they call you to collect at the gate. Waiting time still applies.

## 9. Tracking problems and what to do

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Stuck on "Waiting for restaurant" | Restaurant tablet offline | It auto-cancels after 3 minutes with a full refund. No action needed. |
| Stuck on "Preparing" well past the ready time | Kitchen delay | Crave Assist asks the restaurant automatically after 10 minutes. Contact support for an update. |
| Partner assigned, then a different partner appears | Reassignment (vehicle issue, partner too far, partner unwell) | Normal. The ETA updates. |
| Map not moving for 10+ minutes | Partner stopped, poor network or GPS problem | Call the partner. If they don't answer, contact support. |
| Partner going the wrong way | Batched order, one-way roads or road closure | Call the partner or share directions. |
| "Delivered" but not received | Wrong door, handed to someone else, or wrongly marked | Report within 24 hours: Help > select order > "Order not received". |
| Can't call the partner | Masked number busy, or partner in a no-signal area | Use in-app chat, or ask support to contact the partner. |

## 10. Safety

- If you feel unsafe during a delivery, or a partner behaves inappropriately, use **Help > Report a safety issue**. This goes straight to the Trust & Safety team, which responds within **5 minutes**, 24x7.
- If there is an immediate danger, call **112** (India's emergency number) first.
- Partners in an accident during delivery use their own emergency button. Your order is reassigned or cancelled with a full refund.

## 11. Privacy

- The partner's live location is shown to the customer only while the order is active (from pickup to delivery).
- After delivery, the customer's exact address and phone number are hidden from the partner's app.
- Delivery photos for contactless orders are kept for 30 days for dispute resolution and then deleted.
