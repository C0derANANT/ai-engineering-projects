# How to Cancel an Order

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Consumer Product team · See also: `policies/cancellation-policy.md` for the full rules on fees and exceptions_

This is a how-to guide for customers and support agents. For the fees and refunds that apply at each stage, see the cancellation policy.

## 1. Quick answer

| When you cancel | How | What you get back |
|---|---|---|
| Within 60 seconds of placing | Tracking screen > **Cancel order** | Full refund |
| Before the restaurant accepts | Tracking screen > **Cancel order** | Full refund |
| After acceptance, before "Preparing" | Help > select order > "Cancel order" | Refund minus a cancellation fee of 50% of item total and packaging |
| After "Preparing" starts | Help > select order > "Cancel order" | No refund (100% fee), unless an exception applies |
| Order more than 30 min past the promised time | Tracking screen > **Cancel for free** (button appears automatically) | Full refund |
| Scheduled order, more than 60 min before slot | Orders > Scheduled > **Cancel** | Full refund |

## 2. Cancelling in the app

### 2.1 During the grace period or before acceptance

1. Open the order's tracking screen.
2. Tap **Cancel order**.
3. Choose a reason (optional but helpful): "Ordered by mistake", "Wrong address", "Want to change items", "Taking too long", "Other".
4. Confirm. The screen shows "Order cancelled. ₹X will be refunded to <method>."

The refund starts immediately. See `policies/refund-policy.md` for timelines by payment method.

### 2.2 After the restaurant accepts

The **Cancel order** button is replaced by **Need help?**. To cancel:

1. Tap **Need help?** (or Help > select order).
2. Choose **"I want to cancel"**.
3. Crave Assist shows the exact fee that applies right now, for example: "Cancelling now will cost ₹215 (50% of item total and packaging). You'll get ₹243 back."
4. Confirm to cancel, or choose "Keep my order".

The fee is calculated at the moment you confirm. If the restaurant moves to "Preparing" while you are deciding, the higher fee applies. The app warns you if this happens.

### 2.3 Free cancellation for very late orders

If an order has not been delivered **30 minutes after the promised delivery time** (the upper end of the ETA shown at checkout), a **Cancel for free** banner appears on the tracking screen. Tapping it cancels the order with a full refund, including fees and tip, whatever stage the order is at. This applies to all customers, not only Plus members.

**Example:** checkout showed "35–40 min" at 8:00 pm, so the promised time was 8:40 pm. If the order has not arrived by 9:10 pm, you can cancel for free.

### 2.4 Free cancellation when no delivery partner is assigned

If the food has been ready for **20 minutes** and no delivery partner has been assigned, the app offers a free cancellation. This is rare and usually happens during sudden storms or app-wide incidents.

## 3. Cancelling a Scheduled Order

- Orders > Scheduled > select order > **Cancel**.
- Free until **60 minutes before the slot starts**.
- Within 60 minutes of the slot, the restaurant may already be preparing, so the stage-based fees apply.

## 4. Cancelling a Group Cart order

- Only the host can cancel.
- Participants who want out must ask the host to remove their items **before** the order is placed.

## 5. Cancelling a COD order

- COD orders follow the same stages and fees.
- Because nothing has been paid, any cancellation fee becomes **outstanding dues** on your account. They are added to your next order, which must be paid online.
- Refusing a COD order at the door counts as a cancellation at the "Picked up" stage (100% fee). Two refused or unpaid COD orders within 90 days disable COD on the account.

## 6. When CraveCart or the restaurant cancels

You are not charged anything if the cancellation is caused by:

- the restaurant not accepting within 3 minutes,
- the restaurant rejecting the order (item out of stock, kitchen closing, too busy),
- the restaurant cancelling after accepting,
- CraveCart pausing service in your area (heavy rain, flooding, curfew),
- a delivery partner accident or vehicle breakdown, where no replacement partner can complete the order,
- a technical failure on CraveCart's side.

You receive a full refund automatically and an in-app notification explaining the reason. If the restaurant cancels **after 10 minutes or more of preparation time**, support may add an apology coupon at its discretion.

## 7. What happens to coupons, CraveCart Money and tips

- **Coupon:** restored if the cancellation is free (grace period, before acceptance, or caused by the restaurant or CraveCart) and the coupon is still valid. Otherwise it is used up.
- **CraveCart Money:** any portion paid with CraveCart Money is refunded to CraveCart Money instantly.
- **Tip:** refunded in full if the order is cancelled before pickup or cancelled for free. Not refunded if the order was picked up and then cancelled by the customer (except in the late-order and no-partner cases above).
- **Plus On-Time Promise credit:** not given for orders the customer cancels.

## 8. Common questions

**Q: I cancelled within 30 seconds but was charged. Why?**
A: You should not have been charged. Check the cancellation screen. If it shows a fee, contact support with the order ID; the fee will be reversed. Occasionally, the bank shows a pending debit that is released automatically within 5 to 7 business days.

**Q: The restaurant accepted in 10 seconds, before I could cancel. Can I still cancel for free?**
A: The 60-second grace period still applies. Cancellation is free during the first 60 seconds, even if the restaurant has already accepted.

**Q: I entered the wrong address. Do I have to cancel?**
A: Not necessarily. You can often change the address instead. See `ordering/modifying-an-order.md`.

**Q: Can support waive the cancellation fee?**
A: Agents can waive it only in the situations listed in the cancellation policy's exceptions, or with team lead approval in documented special circumstances, such as a medical emergency. A first-time paid cancellation for a long-standing customer may be considered at the agent's discretion, once per 12 months.
