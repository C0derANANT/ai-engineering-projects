# Delivery Times: How ETAs Work and What Happens When Orders Are Late

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Logistics and Dispatch team_

## 1. Typical delivery times

- **National average (Jan–Oct 2026):** 31 minutes from order placed to delivered.
- **Typical range:** 25 to 40 minutes for restaurants within 5 km.
- **Fastest zones:** central Bengaluru, central Hyderabad and Gurugram (average 26 to 28 minutes).
- **Slowest periods:** weekday dinner peak (8:00 to 9:30 pm), Friday and Saturday nights, heavy rain, festival evenings and big cricket matches.

## 2. How the ETA is calculated

The ETA shown before you order is a range, for example **"30–35 min"**. It is built from four parts:

| Part | What it is | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| Acceptance time | Time for the restaurant to accept | 0.5–3 min |
| Preparation time | Restaurant's historical prep time for these items at this hour | 8–25 min |
| Pickup wait | Time for a delivery partner to arrive and collect | 0–5 min (overlaps with preparation) |
| Travel time | Road distance and live traffic from the restaurant to you | 8–20 min |

A buffer of 2 to 5 minutes is added depending on how reliable the restaurant and the area are. The model is retrained weekly on recent orders.

**The promised delivery time** is the **upper end** of the range shown at checkout, counted from when the order was placed. If checkout showed "30–35 min" at 7:00 pm, the promised time is 7:35 pm.

## 3. Why the ETA can change after ordering

The tracking screen updates the ETA live. It can move later because:

- The restaurant is taking longer than usual (a large order, a busy kitchen, items cooked fresh to order such as dum biryani or pizza).
- Few delivery partners are available nearby (rain, peak hours).
- Traffic, road closures, processions or waterlogging on the route.
- The delivery partner is completing a batched order (see section 6).
- The customer changed the address after ordering.

The ETA can also move **earlier** if the food is ready and picked up sooner than expected.

> The promised time used for compensation and free cancellation is always the one shown **at checkout**, not a later updated ETA.

## 4. Delivery partner assignment

- CraveCart assigns a partner so they arrive at the restaurant about when the food is ready, to avoid long waits that cool the food.
- Most orders have a partner assigned within 5 minutes of the restaurant accepting.
- If no partner has been assigned **20 minutes after the food is marked ready**, the customer is offered a free cancellation. Dispatch also raises the trip fee for nearby partners to attract one.

## 5. When an order is late

| Delay beyond promised time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1–10 minutes | No action. The live ETA is shown and support can share updates. |
| More than 10 minutes (Plus members) | **On-Time Promise:** ₹50 CraveCart Money credited automatically within 24 hours (maximum 4 per month; exclusions apply). |
| More than 20 minutes | Crave Assist proactively messages the customer with the reason and the new ETA. |
| More than 30 minutes (all customers) | A **Cancel for free** banner appears. The customer can cancel for a full refund at any stage. |
| More than 45 minutes, order still delivered | Support may add an apology coupon on request (usually ₹75 off, valid 7 days), at the agent's discretion. |

**On-Time Promise exclusions:** Scheduled Orders, Group Cart orders, orders where the address was changed after placing, delays caused by the customer being unreachable or giving wrong directions, and periods with an active severe-weather high-demand fee.

**Examples**

1. *Plus member, promised 8:40 pm, delivered 8:52 pm (12 minutes late):* eligible for ₹50 CraveCart Money under the On-Time Promise.
2. *Non-Plus customer, promised 8:40 pm, still not delivered at 9:12 pm:* can tap "Cancel for free" for a full refund. If they choose to wait, they are not charged extra, but no automatic credit is given.
3. *Plus member, heavy rain with high-demand fee active, 25 minutes late:* not eligible for the On-Time Promise (severe weather exclusion), but can cancel for free after 30 minutes late.

## 6. Batched orders

- In busy areas, a delivery partner may carry **two orders** from the same or nearby restaurants to customers in the same direction.
- Batching is used only when the predicted extra time for each customer is **5 minutes or less**, and the ETA shown at checkout already includes it.
- Orders from Plus members and orders with an item total over ₹1,000 are never batched with more than one other order.

## 7. Scheduled Orders

- Delivery is targeted within the chosen 15-minute slot, for example 1:00–1:15 pm.
- The restaurant begins about 40 minutes before the slot.
- Scheduled Orders are not covered by the On-Time Promise. If a scheduled order arrives **more than 30 minutes after the slot ends**, the customer can cancel for free, and support may offer an apology coupon.

## 8. Weather, festivals and service pauses

- **Heavy rain:** ETAs widen automatically, a high-demand fee may apply, and some restaurants turn off delivery. Delivery partners are told not to ride through waterlogged roads. Safety comes before speed.
- **Red-alert weather, floods and curfews:** service in the affected zone is paused. Placed orders are cancelled with a full refund if they cannot be completed safely.
- **Festivals (Diwali, New Year's Eve, Eid, Durga Puja):** expect longer prep times. CraveCart shows a banner warning of delays.

## 9. Late-night delivery

- Where late-night service exists (11:00 pm to 5:59 am), ETAs are usually 5 to 10 minutes longer because fewer restaurants and partners are active.
- A ₹15 late-night fee applies.

## 10. Late-delivery FAQs

**Q: My order is 15 minutes late and I'm not a Plus member. Do I get anything?**
A: There is no automatic credit for non-members until the order is 30 minutes late, when you can cancel for free. You can still contact support. Agents can share the exact reason and may offer goodwill credit in exceptional cases.

**Q: The ETA jumped from 25 to 50 minutes after I ordered. Can I cancel?**
A: If it's still within 60 seconds of ordering, or the restaurant has not accepted yet, yes, for free. Otherwise, standard cancellation fees apply until the order is 30 minutes past the promised time shown at checkout.

**Q: The app shows "Delivered" but I didn't receive it.**
A: Report it within 24 hours from Help > select order > "Order not received". See `policies/refund-policy.md`.

**Q: Why is my delivery partner moving away from my location?**
A: They may be collecting a batched order, taking a one-way route or avoiding a closed road. If the partner has not moved for 10 minutes or more, contact support.
