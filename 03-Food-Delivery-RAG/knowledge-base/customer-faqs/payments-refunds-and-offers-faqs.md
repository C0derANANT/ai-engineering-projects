# Customer FAQs: Payments, Refunds, Offers and CraveCart Plus

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Customer Experience · Detailed rules: `policies/refund-policy.md`, `company/pricing-fees-and-membership.md`_

## Payments

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: UPI, credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), net banking, selected wallets, CraveCart Money, Pay Later through partners, and Cash on Delivery for orders up to ₹2,000.

**Q: Is it safe to save my card?**
A: Yes. Cards are saved as tokens under RBI rules. CraveCart never stores your full card number or CVV.

**Q: Money was deducted but my order wasn't placed. What now?**
A: Don't pay again straight away. If no order was created, your bank reverses the amount automatically, usually within 5 to 7 business days. If it hasn't come back after 7 business days, contact support with the UPI transaction ID or bank reference.

**Q: I was charged twice for one order.**
A: Contact support within 30 days with the order ID and your bank or UPI reference. The extra charge is refunded within 48 hours of verification.

**Q: My payment shows "pending". Should I try again?**
A: Wait up to 15 minutes. If the bank confirms in that time, your order goes through. If not, the order isn't placed and any debit is reversed. Retrying many times can put temporary holds on your funds.

**Q: Why isn't Cash on Delivery available for me?**
A: COD isn't available for orders over ₹2,000, Scheduled Orders or Group Cart orders. It's also turned off for accounts with outstanding dues or with 2 refused or unpaid COD orders in the last 90 days (for 180 days).

**Q: Can I switch from COD to online payment?**
A: Yes, at any time before delivery. Tap "Pay online now" on the tracking screen.

**Q: How do I get a GST invoice?**
A: Orders > select order > "Download invoice". For business invoices, add your GSTIN in Profile > Business details **before** placing the order. It can't be added afterwards.

## Fees

**Q: What is the platform fee?**
A: A flat ₹6 per order for all customers, including Plus members. It supports app operations, payment processing and 24x7 support.

**Q: How is the delivery fee calculated?**
A: ₹20 for the first 3 km, then ₹8 per km, capped at ₹70. Plus members get free delivery on orders of ₹199 or more from restaurants within 7 km.

**Q: Why was there a "high-demand" fee?**
A: When demand is much higher than the number of available delivery partners (heavy rain, festivals, big matches), a fee of ₹10 to ₹40 may apply. It's always shown before you pay, with the reason. For Plus members it's capped at ₹20.

**Q: Why do menu prices differ from the restaurant's dine-in prices?**
A: Restaurants set their own menu prices on CraveCart, and these may be different from dine-in prices.

## Refunds

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
A:
- CraveCart Money: instant (within 10 minutes)
- UPI and wallets: 1–3 business days
- Pay Later: 3–5 business days
- Debit or credit card, and net banking: 5–7 business days
- COD orders: instantly to CraveCart Money, or 3–5 business days to your UPI ID or bank account

You can always choose to receive a refund instantly in CraveCart Money instead.

**Q: My refund says "processed" but I don't see it.**
A: Banks can take the full time listed above. A refund reference number (ARN/RRN) appears in Orders > select order > "Refund status" within 48 hours. Share it with your bank. If the money still hasn't arrived 2 business days after the expected time, contact support with the reference.

**Q: How much do I get back for a missing item if I used a coupon?**
A: The item price plus its share of packaging and GST, minus its share of the coupon discount. Example: if a ₹300 item was 37.5% of your item total and you had a ₹120 discount, the discount share is ₹45, so the item part of the refund is ₹270 + its packaging share + GST.

**Q: Is the delivery fee refunded when an item is missing?**
A: Only when the whole order is refunded. For a single missing item, only that item's value (with its packaging and GST share) is refunded.

**Q: Is my tip refunded?**
A: Yes, if the order is cancelled before pickup, cancelled for free (very late, or no delivery partner), or not delivered because of an issue on our side. Tips aren't refunded once an order is delivered.

**Q: What isn't refundable?**
A: Taste preferences when the food matches its description, issues reported after the reporting window, orders that couldn't be delivered because the customer was unreachable or gave a wrong address, correctly charged cancellation fees, and tips on delivered orders.

**Q: Why was my refund not instant this time?**
A: Accounts with more than 4 refund claims, or more than ₹3,000 in claims, in 30 days go to a manual review (completed within 48 hours). This protects restaurants and genuine customers from misuse.

**Q: I disagree with a refund decision. What can I do?**
A: Reply to the support chat to ask for a review by a team lead. If it's still unresolved, write to the Grievance Officer at `grievance@cravecart.example`. You'll get an acknowledgement within 48 hours and a resolution within one month.

## CraveCart Money

**Q: What is CraveCart Money?**
A: CraveCart's in-app wallet. It holds refunds, cashback and gift card balances, and you can use it on any order.

**Q: Does CraveCart Money expire?**
A: Refunds in CraveCart Money never expire. Promotional balances (cashback, apology credits, On-Time Promise credits) have an expiry date shown in the wallet.

**Q: Can I move CraveCart Money to my bank?**
A: Refund balances can be moved back to the original payment method within 30 days of being credited, through support. Promotional balances can't be withdrawn.

**Q: Is there a limit on the wallet?**
A: Yes, ₹10,000. If a refund would go over the limit, the extra goes to your original payment method.

## Coupons and offers

**Q: Can I use two coupons on one order?**
A: No, one coupon per order. A bank offer can often be applied on top, if the bank's terms allow it.

**Q: Why isn't my coupon working?**
A: Check that your item total (before fees and taxes) meets the minimum, the restaurant and payment method are eligible, the coupon hasn't expired, and you haven't already used it the maximum number of times. New-user coupons work only on your first 3 orders.

**Q: If I cancel, do I get my coupon back?**
A: Yes, if the cancellation was free (within 60 seconds, before the restaurant accepted, or cancelled by the restaurant or CraveCart) and the coupon is still valid. Otherwise, it's used up.

## CraveCart Plus

**Q: How much does Plus cost?**
A: ₹149 for 3 months or ₹449 for 12 months, including GST.

**Q: What do I get with Plus?**
A: Free delivery on orders of ₹199 or more from restaurants within 7 km; up to 30% extra off at Plus partner restaurants; a high-demand fee capped at ₹20; priority support with a callback within 10 minutes; and the On-Time Promise (₹50 CraveCart Money if an eligible order is more than 10 minutes late, up to 4 times a month).

**Q: Do Plus members still pay the platform fee?**
A: Yes. The ₹6 platform fee, packaging charges, the late-night fee and GST still apply.

**Q: Which orders aren't covered by the On-Time Promise?**
A: Scheduled Orders, Group Cart orders, orders where the address was changed after placing, delays caused by you being unreachable, and times when a severe-weather high-demand fee is active.

**Q: Can I cancel Plus and get my money back?**
A: Within 7 days of purchase, if you haven't used any benefit, you get a full refund. Otherwise, it stays active until the end of the period. Auto-renew is off by default and you can turn it on or off at any time.

**Q: Can I share my Plus membership with family?**
A: No. Plus is tied to one account and can't be shared, transferred or paused.
