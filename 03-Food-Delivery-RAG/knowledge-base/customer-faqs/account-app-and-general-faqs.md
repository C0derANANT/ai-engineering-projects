# Customer FAQs: Account, App and General Questions

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Customer Experience · Related: `support/technical-support-procedures.md`, `company/overview.md`_

## About CraveCart

**Q: What is CraveCart?**
A: CraveCart is an Indian food ordering and delivery app, founded in 2016 in Bengaluru. You can order from more than 1,10,000 restaurants across 46 cities and track your delivery live.

**Q: Which cities do you serve?**
A: 46 cities, including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Kochi, Indore and Goa. Availability depends on your exact address, so enter it in the app to check.

**Q: What are your service hours?**
A: In most cities, 6:00 am to 2:00 am, depending on restaurant hours. Selected zones in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad are open 24 hours.

**Q: Are delivery partners CraveCart employees?**
A: No. Delivery partners are independent contractors. They get 100% of the tips you give, and CraveCart provides them with accident insurance and a 24x7 emergency helpline.

**Q: Who cooks the food?**
A: Our restaurant partners. They're independent businesses with valid FSSAI licences, responsible for food quality and hygiene. CraveCart handles ordering, payment, delivery and support.

## Account and login

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Download the app or visit the website, enter your Indian mobile number, and verify it with the OTP. You must be 18 or older.

**Q: I'm not receiving the OTP.**
A: Wait 30 seconds and tap Resend (up to 3 times in 15 minutes), or choose "Get OTP on call". Check that your number is correct and that SMS-blocking or spam filters aren't hiding the message.

**Q: My login is locked.**
A: After 5 wrong OTP attempts, login is locked for 30 minutes (error `CC-AUTH-102`). Please wait and try again. Support can't unlock it early.

**Q: How do I change my registered mobile number?**
A: Profile > Edit profile > Mobile number. You'll need to verify both the old and new numbers with OTPs entered in the app. If you no longer have the old number, contact support. You'll be verified through your registered email and recent order details.

**Q: Can I have two accounts?**
A: Each mobile number can have only one account. Creating multiple accounts to reuse new-user offers is against our Terms of Use.

**Q: I see orders I didn't place.**
A: Contact support immediately and choose "Account security". We'll log out all devices, freeze your wallet and investigate within 24 hours. Never share OTPs with anyone.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Profile > Settings > Privacy > Delete account. There's a 30-day grace period: logging in during that time cancels deletion. Withdraw any refund balance in CraveCart Money first. Promotional balances are forfeited.

**Q: How do I download my data?**
A: Profile > Settings > Privacy > "Download my data". We email a download link within 72 hours. It's valid for 7 days.

## The app

**Q: Which phones are supported?**
A: Android 9.0 or later, and iOS 15 or later. You need app version 12.0 or newer to place orders. The website works on the latest two versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.

**Q: The app keeps crashing.**
A: Update the app, force close and reopen it, and restart your phone. On Android, clear the cache (Settings > Apps > CraveCart > Storage > Clear cache). On iPhone, offload and reinstall the app. Make sure you have at least 300 MB of free space. If it still crashes, contact support with your phone model and app version.

**Q: My location is wrong in the app.**
A: Turn on precise location and Wi-Fi, then edit your saved address and drag the map pin to your exact building entrance. The pin is what the delivery partner follows.

**Q: I'm not getting order notifications.**
A: Make sure notifications are allowed for CraveCart in your phone settings, and that "Order updates" is on in Profile > Settings > Notifications. On Android, turn off battery optimisation for CraveCart. You can turn off promotional notifications without losing order updates.

**Q: What does the error code under an error message mean?**
A: It helps us identify the problem quickly. Common ones: `CC-PAY-302` (payment pending: wait up to 15 minutes), `CC-LOC-201` (address not serviceable), `CC-CART-402` (coupon not applicable) and `CC-NET-601` (internet connection problem). Share the code with support if you need help.

## Support

**Q: How do I contact CraveCart?**
A: The fastest way is in-app chat, available 24x7: Help > select the order > Chat with us. For non-urgent, non-order questions, email `support@cravecart.example`. Plus members can request a callback within 10 minutes.

**Q: Which languages does support offer?**
A: English and Hindi 24x7. Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Malayalam and Punjabi from 9 am to 11 pm.

**Q: Someone called me from "CraveCart" asking for my OTP to give me a refund. Is this real?**
A: No. This is a scam. CraveCart never asks for OTPs, UPI PINs, card details, passwords or the delivery PIN, and never asks you to install screen-sharing apps. Refunds never require you to pay or "receive" money through a UPI request. Report such calls to `security@cravecart.example`.

**Q: I want to escalate a complaint.**
A: Ask the chat agent for a team lead. If it's still unresolved, write to our Grievance Officer at `grievance@cravecart.example`. They acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month.

**Q: How do I report a safety issue with a delivery partner?**
A: Help > Report a safety issue. Our Trust & Safety team responds within 5 minutes, 24x7. If you're in immediate danger, call 112 first.

## Ratings and reviews

**Q: Can I rate the food and the delivery separately?**
A: Yes. After delivery you can rate the restaurant (food) and the delivery partner (delivery experience) separately. Ratings are anonymous to the delivery partner.

**Q: Can I edit my review?**
A: Yes, within 7 days of the order, from Orders > select order > Edit review.

**Q: Why was my review removed?**
A: Reviews are removed if they contain abusive language, personal information, spam, or content unrelated to the order, or if they're from someone with a conflict of interest (for example, the restaurant's staff).

## Restaurants and partners

**Q: How can I list my restaurant on CraveCart?**
A: Visit the "Partner with us" page on the website. You'll need an FSSAI licence, GST registration (if applicable), PAN, a bank account and your menu. Onboarding usually takes 5 to 7 working days.

**Q: How do I become a delivery partner?**
A: Download the CraveCart Delivery Partner app. You'll need a valid driving licence, vehicle registration (for motorbikes and scooters), Aadhaar, PAN and a bank account. Bicycle and e-bike partners are welcome in selected cities.

## Privacy

**Q: Does the delivery partner see my phone number?**
A: No. Calls go through a masked number. After delivery, your exact address and number are hidden from the partner's app.

**Q: How long are delivery photos kept?**
A: Photos taken for "Leave at the door" deliveries are kept for 30 days for dispute resolution and then deleted.

**Q: Does CraveCart sell my data?**
A: No. We process personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, only to provide and improve the service. See the Privacy Policy in the app for details. Requests can be sent to `privacy@cravecart.example`.
