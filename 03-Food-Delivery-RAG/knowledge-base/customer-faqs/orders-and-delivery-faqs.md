# Customer FAQs: Orders and Delivery

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Owner: Customer Experience · These answers summarise the detailed documents in the `ordering` and `policies` folders._

## Placing orders

**Q: Is there a minimum order value?**
A: No. If your item total is below ₹149, a ₹25 small cart fee is added. It disappears once your item total reaches ₹149.

**Q: Can I order from two restaurants in one order?**
A: No. Each order can contain items from only one restaurant. Place separate orders. Each has its own delivery fee and ETA.

**Q: How do I order for someone at a different address?**
A: Add their address, then add their name and phone number under "Receiver details" at checkout. The delivery partner will call them. For orders of ₹1,000 or more, share the 4-digit delivery PIN with them.

**Q: Can I schedule an order for later?**
A: Yes. Choose "Schedule for later" at checkout and pick a 15-minute slot between 30 minutes and 2 days ahead. Scheduled orders must be paid online, and you can change or cancel them for free until 60 minutes before the slot.

**Q: Can I order for a group and let everyone choose their own food?**
A: Yes. Use Group Cart: share the cart link with up to 10 people. Everyone adds their items, and the host pays for and places the order. The link expires after 2 hours.

**Q: What's the largest order I can place?**
A: Up to 20 units of any one item and an item total of up to ₹15,000. For bigger orders, such as office parties, use CraveCart for Business at least 24 hours in advance.

**Q: Can I add cooking instructions like "less spicy"?**
A: Yes, up to 150 characters per order. Restaurants try to follow them but can't always guarantee it. If a dish is marked spicy, "too spicy" alone isn't grounds for a refund.

**Q: Why don't I get cutlery?**
A: Cutlery is off by default to reduce plastic waste. Turn on "Send cutlery" at checkout if you need it.

**Q: Does CraveCart deliver alcohol or medicines?**
A: No. CraveCart only delivers food and beverages from restaurant partners. We don't deliver alcohol, tobacco or medicines.

## Changing and cancelling

**Q: I made a mistake in my order. What can I do?**
A: For the first 60 seconds after ordering, tap **Edit order** to change items, address, payment or coupon, or **Cancel** for a full refund. After that, you can still remove items (but not add them) until the restaurant starts preparing, through Help.

**Q: Can I add an item after placing my order?**
A: Not after the 60-second grace period. Please place a new order. Support can ask the delivery partner to wait if both orders will be ready within 10 minutes of each other, but this isn't guaranteed.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address?**
A: Before pickup, yes, to any address within 2 km of the original that the restaurant delivers to. The delivery fee is recalculated. After pickup, only small changes within 500 m are possible, if the delivery partner agrees.

**Q: How much will I be charged if I cancel?**
A:
- Within 60 seconds of ordering, or before the restaurant accepts: **free**.
- After the restaurant accepts, but before it starts preparing: **50%** of item total and packaging.
- Once the restaurant is preparing, or later: **100%** (no refund, except your tip if not yet picked up).
- If your order is **more than 30 minutes past the promised time**, you can cancel **for free** at any stage.

**Q: The restaurant cancelled my order. Will I be charged?**
A: No. You'll get a full refund automatically. If the restaurant cancelled after preparing for 10 minutes or more, support may also add an apology coupon.

**Q: I refused a COD order at the door. What happens?**
A: It's treated as a 100% cancellation. The order value becomes outstanding dues on your account, to be paid with your next order (online). Two refused or unpaid COD orders within 90 days disable COD for 180 days.

## Delivery times and tracking

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Usually 25 to 40 minutes. The national average in 2026 is 31 minutes. Your exact ETA is shown before you order as a range, such as "30–35 min".

**Q: What is the "promised delivery time"?**
A: The upper end of the ETA range shown at checkout, counted from when you placed the order. It's used for the Plus On-Time Promise and free cancellation of very late orders. It doesn't change if the live ETA changes later.

**Q: My order is late. Do I get compensation?**
A:
- **Plus members:** if an eligible order arrives more than 10 minutes after the promised time, you automatically get ₹50 CraveCart Money within 24 hours (up to 4 times a month).
- **Everyone:** if your order is more than 30 minutes past the promised time, you can cancel for a full refund.
- Support may offer an apology coupon for orders delivered more than 45 minutes late.

**Q: Why did my delivery partner go somewhere else first?**
A: In busy areas, a partner may carry two orders going the same way. We only do this when it adds 5 minutes or less to your time, and your ETA already includes it.

**Q: How do I contact my delivery partner?**
A: Tap Call or Chat on the tracking screen. Calls use a masked number, so neither of you sees the other's real number. Calling works from when the partner is assigned until 30 minutes after delivery.

**Q: What is the delivery PIN?**
A: For orders of ₹1,000 or more, you get a 4-digit PIN. Give it to the partner only when you have your food. CraveCart support will never ask you for this PIN.

**Q: How long will the partner wait if I don't answer?**
A: 7 minutes, with at least two calls. If we still can't reach you, the order is marked undelivered. Prepaid orders aren't refunded in this case, and COD orders count as refused.

**Q: Can the partner leave my food at the door?**
A: Yes. Choose "Leave at the door" in delivery instructions. The partner takes a photo as proof. This isn't available for COD orders or orders with a delivery PIN.

**Q: The app says "Delivered" but I didn't get my order.**
A: Check with family, neighbours or building security first. Then report it within 24 hours: Help > select order > "Order not received". We check the partner's GPS, the PIN or photo, and talk to the partner. If delivery can't be confirmed, you get a full refund.

**Q: Do you deliver late at night?**
A: In selected zones of Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad, yes, 24 hours. A ₹15 late-night fee applies from 11:00 pm to 5:59 am. In other cities, service runs from 6:00 am to 2:00 am, depending on restaurant hours.

**Q: Why can't I order? It says "We don't deliver here yet."**
A: Your address is outside every nearby restaurant's delivery range, or service is temporarily paused in your area (for example, during heavy rain or flooding). Try a nearby address or check back later.

## Problems with delivered orders

**Q: An item was missing. How do I get a refund?**
A: Report it within 3 hours of delivery: Help > select order > "Items missing". Select the item. You'll be refunded the item price plus its share of packaging and GST, minus its share of any discount. Where available, you can choose to have the item sent instead.

**Q: My food arrived spilled or damaged.**
A: Report it within 3 hours with a photo. You'll be refunded for the affected items, or the whole order if all of it was unusable.

**Q: I found something in my food (hair, plastic or an insect).**
A: We're very sorry. Report it within 24 hours with a photo. You'll get a full order refund right away, and our Food Safety team will contact you within 24 hours. Keep the remaining food and packaging if you can.

**Q: I felt ill after eating.**
A: Seek medical help first, and call 112 in an emergency. Then report it within 48 hours. The Food Safety team will investigate. CraveCart may reimburse documented medical costs of up to ₹10,000 after investigation.

**Q: The packaging seal was broken.**
A: Please don't accept the order. Report it immediately for a full refund.

**Q: I got a non-veg item instead of veg.**
A: Report it immediately. You'll get a full refund for the item, and for the whole order if the rest was affected. We treat this as a serious food safety issue, and the restaurant is investigated.
